The **“Playing solo”** section is basically about **why Sandeep Maheshwari has chosen to keep Imagesbazaar independent instead of selling it, bringing in an owner/investor, or building it primarily to be acquired.**

Here’s what he is saying:

### 1. He has a strong emotional attachment to Imagesbazaar

He says:

> “One of the primary reasons to stay independent is the strong emotional connection I've developed with the company over time.”

Imagesbazaar isn't just a business investment to him. He **started it himself and watched it grow from a very small beginning into a large company**.

So there is an element of:

**“I built this myself, I have spent decades on it, and I don't want to give control of it away.”**

---

### 2. He doesn't need someone else to control the company's direction

He says he believes in his own ability to:

- understand changes in the market
- innovate
- respond to technological changes
- deal with competition
- guide the company toward its long-term goals

That's important.

He's essentially saying:

**“I believe I can take Imagesbazaar into the future myself, so I don't need to sell or hand over control to someone else.”**

This is especially relevant because the stock-image industry is changing dramatically because of AI.

Instead of saying:

> “AI is disrupting us, maybe we should sell.”

his attitude appears to be:

> **“AI is changing the industry. I'll adapt the company myself.”**

---

### 3. He isn't following the typical startup → funding → acquisition path

The interesting part is the contrast with the modern startup model.

A lot of startups are built with the eventual objective of:

**Build → Raise money → Grow rapidly → Sell/IPO → Founder exits**

Maheshwari is saying that **isn't his motivation**.

He specifically says:

> “In a modern era where mostly businesses are built with a clear purpose to be taken over…”

But he doesn't want to operate Imagesbazaar that way.

His goal is more:

**Build → Own → Keep control → Grow for the long term**

rather than:

**Build → Sell**

---

### 4. Independence is part of his identity as an entrepreneur

He also says that the difficulties of the last two decades helped him develop personally and professionally.

So for him, the value isn't only the financial value of Imagesbazaar.

It's also the experience of:

**building something → facing problems → solving them → seeing it grow → becoming better himself.**

That's why he says:

> “Two decades later, I can look back on this decision without regret.”

In other words, **he doesn't regret choosing entrepreneurship and continuing to operate independently.**

---

### 5. What “playing solo” really means

It **doesn't mean he's literally running the company alone**.

It means **he is choosing to remain independent as the owner/decision-maker rather than bringing in someone who could take control or eventually acquiring/selling the company.**

Think of it like:

**Typical startup mindset:**

> “How big can I make this so someone will buy it?”

**His mindset:**

> “How big and successful can I make this while continuing to own and control it?”

And that's why this section is interesting in the context of Imagesbazaar's future.

Even though AI is creating enormous uncertainty for stock photography, his response isn't to exit. **He's betting on his ability to adapt Imagesbazaar and continue building it independently.**
