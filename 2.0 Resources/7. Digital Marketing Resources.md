## 1. Google Digital Marketing & E-commerce Professional Certificate

Best overall

Best for: Learning digital marketing from the ground up.

Covers SEO, search advertising, social media, email marketing, analytics, e-commerce, and marketing strategy. Best if you want a structured, comprehensive curriculum.

Cost: Paid Coursera subscription; check the current India price.

[View course](https://www.coursera.org/professional-certificates/google-digital-marketing-ecommerce?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 2. HubSpot Academy Digital Marketing Certification

Best free starting point

Best for: Content strategy, SEO, inbound marketing, and lead generation.

A practical introduction to attracting an audience organically and building a marketing strategy without spending much money.

Cost: Free, including certification.

[View course](https://academy.hubspot.com/courses/digital-marketing?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 3. Google Skillshop — Google Ads and Analytics

Best for: Measurable growth and paid advertising.

Learn Google Ads, campaign measurement, and analytics. Particularly valuable if you want to understand how businesses acquire customers and evaluate campaign performance.

Cost: Free training and available certifications.

[View Skillshop](https://skillshop.withgoogle.com/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 4. Semrush Academy SEO courses

Best for: Organic traffic, website optimization, and search visibility.

Learn keyword research, competitor analysis, SEO audits, and content optimization. Useful if you want to grow a website, blog, or cybersecurity portfolio.

Cost: Free courses and certificates.

[View courses](https://www.semrush.com/academy/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 5. Meta Blueprint

Best for: Facebook and Instagram marketing.

Covers paid social campaigns, audience targeting, creative testing, and campaign optimization. More useful if you plan to manage social media advertisements.

Cost: Learning materials are free; some certification exams are paid.

[View courses](https://www.facebook.com/business/learn?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 6. Ahrefs Academy

Best for: Technical SEO and content-led growth.

A good specialist option for understanding backlinks, search intent, keyword research, and organic search growth.

Cost: Many courses are free.

[View courses](https://ahrefs.com/academy?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## 7. The Complete Digital Marketing Course

Best for: Budget-friendly, broad practical exposure.
