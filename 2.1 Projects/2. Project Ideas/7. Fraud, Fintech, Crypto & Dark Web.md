### Develop Credit Risk Management Model using alternative data
This leads to a large part of the population in most emerging markets failing to access banking channels and consequently remaining unbanked or underbanked, effectively denying lenders a gauge of their creditworthiness. Traditional credit scoring models depend on historical financial data like credit reports and bank statements, excluding persons and small businesses with limited financial track records. Fintech companies are starting to use alternative sources — social media activity, mobile phone usage, utility payments — to determine credit risk far more accurately than traditional methods.
Traditional credit scoring models are inadequate for assessing the creditworthiness of individuals and businesses without extensive credit histories.
**Desired Outcome:** A new credit risk model that uses alternative data sources to improve accuracy and representation of credit assessments. It needs to be transparent, scalable, and compliant with data privacy regulations, allowing lenders to extend credit to under-credited populations with minimal risk.

### Impersonated Registration/Frauds in Mobile & Internet Banking + behaviour-based passwordless authentication
Registrations by fraudsters impersonating customers in mobile & internet banking applications is a major security concern. Fraudsters impersonate bank, courier, customs or police officials and send dubious links to customers to install apps, then initiate mobile banking registration on the fraudster's device and obtain OTP, debit card details, or other authentication details. After successful registration they transfer funds to the fraudster's account.
- Common techniques: SIM swap fraud, vishing (voice phishing), email scams. Fraudsters send fake emails, SMS (smishing), or calls (vishing) impersonating the bank to trick users into sharing credentials, OTPs, or account numbers. Once obtained, they conduct unauthorized transactions.
- As per current SIM binding process, mobile number, IP, device id etc. is captured.

Behavior-Based Authentication monitors and analyzes the unique behavioural traits of users. By continually verifying the user throughout their session, it prevents many forms of fraud including account takeovers, in a dynamic, secure, user-friendly way. However, optimum balance of security, user convenience, and privacy is not achieved yet. Behaviour-based adaptive authentication is nascent and rule-based technologies lack behaviour-based flexibility — a risk score based on customer behaviour including behavioural biometrics and anomaly detection is required.

**Desired Outcome:** Provide solution to banks to differentiate between actual customer vs. fraudster-initiated mobile/internet banking registration.
- Advanced fraud detection algorithms to detect suspicious activity indicating a cloned SIM card / dual SIM card etc.
- Algorithm to identify distance/difference between current location of customer (basis location of mobile number/SIM) and current location of person initiating the registration.
- Set up alerts for suspicious activities — login attempts from new locations/devices, multiple failed logins, changes to account details.
- Implement strong authentication methods that cannot be bypassed even if phishing is successful.
- Notify users immediately if a SIM swap is detected or if suspicious activity occurs.
- Solution must provide an alternative to traditional authentication factors (now being exploited) to enable password-less login.

### Mule Accounts & Collusive Fraud in UPI (Fintech Security incl. Blockchain)
Develop a real-time fraud detection system to identify mule accounts and collusive fraud networks in UPI and instant payment platforms, leveraging graph-based transaction analytics, device fingerprinting, identity attribute correlation, and real-time risk scoring APIs that detect fraudulent clusters early, minimize false positives, and integrate with India's high-volume payment infrastructure.
**Exact Deliverables:**
- Prototype for detecting mule accounts using transaction graph features and device fingerprints.
- Model for spotting collusive fraud patterns (money-mule rings) across UPI flows.
- Precision/recall evaluation report on fraud detection performance.
- Dashboard or visualization layer to track suspicious clusters of accounts.
**Milestones, Evolution Parameters:**
- Phase 1: Build baseline fraud detection using transaction rules.
- Phase 2: Implement graph-based anomaly detection and device fingerprinting.
- Phase 3: Deploy real-time API for risk scoring in simulated UPI flows.
- KPIs: Detection accuracy, false positive rate, average detection latency, scalability to millions of transactions.
**Additional Information:** Emphasize scalability and integration with real-time payment rails; explore fraud graph learning and behavioral analysis; visualization of fraud networks enhances usability for investigators.
