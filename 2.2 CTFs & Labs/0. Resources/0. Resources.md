## ctf resources

|thing|my take|
|---|---|
|★ [picoCTF](https://picoctf.org/)|carnegie mellon's beginner CTF, online all year. the single best on-ramp. gentle, well-made, start here.|
|[CTFtime](https://ctftime.org/)|the calendar of every CTF on earth, plus team rankings and, crucially, the writeups afterward.|
|[Awesome CTF](https://github.com/apsdehal/awesome-ctf)|a massive curated list of tools/resources for when you need a thing for category X.|
|[CTF Field Guide](https://trailofbits.github.io/ctf/)|Trail of Bits' intro to how the whole circus actually works.|

----

### wargames: always-on, learn-at-your-pace

CTFs are time-boxed events. wargames just sit there forever, level by level, perfect for actually learning.

|thing|my take|
|---|---|
|★ [OverTheWire](https://overthewire.org/wargames/)|Bandit → Leviathan → Narnia is a genuine rite of passage. do Bandit no matter what.|
|[Exploit Education](https://exploit.education/)|the friendliest baby-steps intro to memory corruption that exists.|
|★ [Microcorruption](https://microcorruption.com/)|an embedded/assembly CTF that runs in your browser, zero setup, weirdly addictive. i lost a whole weekend to this once.|
|[Root-Me](https://www.root-me.org/)|hundreds of tiny challenges across every category.|

-----

### the little ctf-goblin toolkit

stuff you'll reach for in basically every comp, whatever the flavour.

|tool|what it's for|
|---|---|
|★ [CyberChef](https://gchq.github.io/CyberChef/)|encodings, ciphers, hashing, the magic wand, all drag-and-drop. you'll use it constantly.|
|[Burp Suite](https://portswigger.net/burp)|intercept / modify / replay HTTP. the web hacker's primary weapon.|
|[nmap](https://nmap.org/)|what's even running on this host.|
|[ffuf](https://github.com/ffuf/ffuf) / [gobuster](https://github.com/OJ/gobuster)|fuzz for hidden paths, files and params.|
|[hashcat](https://hashcat.net/hashcat/) / [John](https://www.openwall.com/john/)|crack hashes and passwords.|
|[pwndbg](https://github.com/pwndbg/pwndbg) / [GEF](https://github.com/hugsy/gef)|make gdb actually usable for exploitation.|

-----

### people who explain their work

fastest way to get better at CTFs: watch good players think out loud.

|who|what|
|---|---|
|★ [IppSec](https://www.youtube.com/@ippsec)|detailed HTB walkthroughs. [ippsec.rocks](https://ippsec.rocks/) lets you search his videos by technique, which is genuinely wild.|
|[John Hammond](https://www.youtube.com/@_JohnHammond)|CTF solves, malware, general hacking. approachable, prolific, comfy to learn alongside.|
|[LiveOverflow](https://www.youtube.com/@LiveOverflow)|the "but how does it ACTUALLY work" deep dives.|
|CTFtime writeups|after a comp, read how the people who solved it solved it. this is where the real learning hides.|
