# OSINT & Social Media Intelligence

Open-source intelligence, social-media monitoring, profiling, and intelligence fusion.

---

## Profiling & footprinting
- **Social Media Profile Analyzer** — Collect publicly available information from social media profiles, generate investigation summary reports, identify usernames used across platforms, create relationship mapping.
- **Username Intelligence Tool** — Search a username across 100+ platforms, check account existence, generate digital footprint report. _Use Case: Suspect profiling._
- **Fake Profile Investigation Toolkit**
- **Missing Person OSINT Tool** — Collect publicly available traces, face matching (demo environment), social media correlation.

## Monitoring & dashboards
- **Social Media Intelligence Dashboard** — Public profile monitoring, keyword alerts, trend mapping. Tech: OSINT tools.
- **Build a Social Media Scam Trend Tracker**
- **AI-Powered Social Media Threat Intelligence Platform** — Monitor public content for fraud campaigns, fake investment schemes, and impersonation scams.
- **Telegram Channel Monitoring Tool** — Monitor public channels, detect scam groups, keyword-based alerts. _Use Case: Cybercrime intelligence._
- **Telegram Groups: How to Infiltrate and How to Find These Groups** — who are doing illegal things.
- **Telegram Channel Ban**
- **Instagram Channel Ban**

## Toolkits & fusion platforms
- **OSINT Toolkit for Law Enforcement** — Compile free tools for username search, domain analysis, metadata extraction, social media investigations. _Output: investigator's handbook._
- **Cyber Intelligence Fusion Platform** — Integrate OSINT, SOCMINT, Fraud indicators, Domain intelligence, Crypto intelligence into a single investigation dashboard.
