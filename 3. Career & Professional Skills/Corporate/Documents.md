
**Essential Documents for Job Switching**

The sources list documents you should collect before leaving a job to avoid HR or salary issues later:
- **Experience Letter:** Official proof that you worked there for background verification.
- **Relieving Letter:** Confirms you left on good terms.
- **Last 3 Months' Salary Slips:** Necessary for background checks and salary negotiations.
- **Form 16 / Tax Documents:** Required for tax filing and switching payroll.
- **PF & UAN Details:** Needed for tracking and transferring Provident Fund (PF) numbers.
- **Increment / Promotion Letters:** Proves career growth for verification or loan documents.
- **Clearance Letter:** Shows you returned all assets and cleared all dues.
