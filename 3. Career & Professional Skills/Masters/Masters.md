the Information Networking Institute (INI) is **not a separate university** from Carnegie Mellon University.
It is a **department/institute inside Carnegie Mellon University**, mainly under the College of Engineering.
Think of it like this:
- **Carnegie Mellon University (CMU)** = whole university
- **School of Computer Science (SCS)** = one school inside CMU
- **Information Networking Institute (INI)** = another institute/program inside CMU Engineering
