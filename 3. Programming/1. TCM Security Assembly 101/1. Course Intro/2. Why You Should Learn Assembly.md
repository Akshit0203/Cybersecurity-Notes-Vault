
# Why You Should Learn Assembly

1. **Understand how computers actually work** at the lowest level — many CS, programming, and cybersecurity concepts "click" once you understand how processors work
2. **Better understand malware** — both for analysis / reverse engineering and for writing your own malware or shellcode
3. **Become a better programmer** even in high-level languages, by knowing what happens when your code is compiled or interpreted
4. **IoT & embedded systems** — extremely valuable for writing and understanding firmware
5. **It's fun, challenging, and fascinating**
6. When the robot overlords overthrow us, it will be valuable to speak their native tongue 🤖
