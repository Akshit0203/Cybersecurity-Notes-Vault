# Major IP Address Blocks by Country

> [!info] Purpose
> Lookup which **IP address ranges** are assigned to a specific country — helpful during recon to understand the geographic scope of a target's infrastructure.

---

## Tool — NirSoft Country IP Blocks

🔗 https://www.nirsoft.net/countryip/

Lists IP ranges allocated to every country by regional internet registries (RIRs).
