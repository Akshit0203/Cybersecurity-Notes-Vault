
### Active Directory Checklist:

Complete all the following steps to be well prepared:

1. Watch & redo every machine you can from our AD videos, especially from our AD Sets/Chains as they simulate relevant AD scenarios on the OSCP Exam effectively. Take mental notes on the approach and methodology used in the videos.
    
2. For volume training on standalone AD machines - do at least 20 machines under the “Active Directory and Networks” section from the Lainkusanagi list below. Not as directly applicable to OSCP Exam as AD Chains/Sets, but still useful for general volume training. The inevitable missing techniques will be filled in as you complete this course.
    
3. Watch every resource linked at the bottom of the Docs here: [https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qnhQj-qUvBrf_XkXhuE7YRauKaMj6pylfSjQiyYNGpU/edit?gid=585521290#gid=585521290](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qnhQj-qUvBrf_XkXhuE7YRauKaMj6pylfSjQiyYNGpU/edit?gid=585521290#gid=585521290)
    
4. Watch & preferable do all the Active Directory Sets/Chains in this course, and do all Active Directory Sets/Chains from Offsec Challenge Labs as well.
    
5. Complete [OSCP A, B, and C](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/4547917816468-OffSec-OSCP-Exam-with-AD-Preparation-Newly-Updated#h_01FX54C5J9T7VJ277R57AS9GS7) AD Sets/Chains from offsec Challenge Labs.
    
6. Work on gaining “Basic Competence” or “Proficient” status on every attack listed in the “Active Directory” section of [Hack Academy OSCP Checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qnhQj-qUvBrf_XkXhuE7YRauKaMj6pylfSjQiyYNGpU/edit?gid=585521290#gid=585521290). This will take some time. It’s okay if this is the last thing you complete.
    
7. Make sure you’ve made notes on every relevant attack you’ll need in the future. Having good notes that works for you is incredibly important.
    
8. If you’ve completed the above, your Active Directory knowledge is now sufficient to start practicing in the OSCP labs, and shortly thereafter do the exam.
