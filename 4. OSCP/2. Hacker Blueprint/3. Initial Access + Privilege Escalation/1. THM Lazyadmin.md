For OSCP, you don't need to manually hide 404 responses in Gobuster because 404 is normally excluded by default.

Why?

- 404 Not Found: Usually means the path doesn't exist.
    
- 200 OK: Potentially valid page or resource.
    
- 301/302: Redirects; investigate the destination.
    
- 403 Forbidden: May indicate an existing resource you don't have permission to access.
    

OSCP tip: Don't ignore 403 responses—they can reveal useful directories even when access is denied.
