
### Initial Access Checklist:

Complete all the following steps to be well prepared:

1. Watch & redo every machine you can from our Initial Access videos. Take mental notes on the approach and methodology used in the videos.
    
2. Do at least 30 machines total for Windows & Linux from the [Lainkusanagi](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18weuz_Eeynr6sXFQ87Cd5F0slOj9Z6rt/edit) or [Netsecfocus](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/u/1/d/1dwSMIAPIam0PuRBkCiDI88pU3yzrqqHkDtBngUHNCw8/htmlview) list below.
    
3. Do at least 20 machines more specifically from [“Proving Grounds Practice”](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/18weuz_Eeynr6sXFQ87Cd5F0slOj9Z6rt/edit?gid=487240997#gid=487240997). These machines are made from offsec and are great practice for Initial Access. This does not apply to “Proving Grounds Play”.
    
4. You have gained “Basic Competence” or “Proficient” status on every attack listed in the “Initial Access” section of [Hack Academy OSCP Checklist](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1qnhQj-qUvBrf_XkXhuE7YRauKaMj6pylfSjQiyYNGpU/edit?gid=1748984591#gid=1748984591). This will take some time. It’s okay if this is the last thing you complete.
    
5. Make sure you’ve made notes on every relevant attack you’ll need in the future.
    
6. If you’ve completed the above, your Initial Access knowledge is now sufficient to start practicing in the OSCP labs, and shortly thereafter do the exam.
