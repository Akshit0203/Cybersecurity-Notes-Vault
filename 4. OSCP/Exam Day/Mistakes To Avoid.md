
These are common and simple mistakes to avoid on the Exam(Read **Exam Advice** for all):

- When taking proof screenshots of obtaining a flag, make sure to show the ip address as well as the flag [in the same screenshot.](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide#screenshot-requirements)
- Obtaining flags **without** an interactive shell(winrm,rdp,reverse shell,psexec etc) will **not** count as valid. So if you obtain a flag through LFI without having an interactive shell as that user, [it will not count.](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide#exam-proofs) So any interactive shell like these (winrm,rdp,reverse shell,psexec etc) are perfectly valid.
- Make sure to note down **all evidence** for the report before it's too late. You will lose access to the OSCP Exam lab after 24 hours, and then its too late to collect those screenshots or outputs you'd need to have to pass the report.
- Make sure to quickly re-exploit all the targets after you've done it once and gotten your points. This is to ensure your notes and screenshots are sufficient enough to effectively reproduce those attacks. This is very important for the reporting segment. If you can't do it with your notes alone, take better notes before the exam lab time is gone.
- Know what tools and actions are [not allowed on the exam.](https://help.offsec.com/hc/en-us/articles/360040165632-OSCP-Exam-Guide#exam-restrictions)
