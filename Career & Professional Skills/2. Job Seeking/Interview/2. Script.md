**Interviewer:** "Tell me about a time you solved a difficult problem."

"During one of my internships, I was assigned a task from a BFSI client to analyze GitHub repositories and their historical commits for payment-related information and identify any potential sensitive data leaks. The challenge was that I had only **one day** to complete it.

My first step was to ask people within the company how to approach it. I spoke with multiple senior team members, but almost everyone told me that completing such an analysis within the given timeframe wasn't possible.

Instead of accepting that, I changed my approach. Rather than asking one person for the entire solution, I asked different people about different parts of the problem. One person helped me understand one step, another explained another component, and I kept collecting small pieces of information.

I then combined those individual steps into a complete workflow, executed it methodically, tested the results, and successfully completed the task within the one-day deadline.

That experience taught me that when something seems impossible, breaking it into smaller problems and learning from multiple people can turn it into something achievable. It also reinforced the importance of persistence, structured thinking, and execution under pressure."
