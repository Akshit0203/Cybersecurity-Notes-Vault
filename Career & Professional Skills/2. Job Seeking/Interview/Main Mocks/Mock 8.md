
## BEHAVIORAL QUESTIONS

1. Describe two specific goals you set for yourself and how successful you were in meeting them. What factors led to your success?
2. Tell me about a time when you failed to meet a deadline. What did you fail to do? What did you learn?

---

## IMPLIED FOLLOW-UP QUESTIONS (VERY IMPORTANT)

3. What were your objectives in that situation?
4. Why did you choose those particular goals?
5. How did you track your progress?
6. What obstacles did you face?
7. What did you learn from that experience?
8. How did you apply that learning later?

---

## GENERIC BEHAVIORAL QUESTION PATTERNS

9. Tell me about a time when you handled a challenge.
10. Give me an example of a situation where you showed leadership.
11. Describe a decision you made and how you made it.

---

## SITUATIONAL QUESTIONS

12. Imagine you are in charge of organizing the grand opening of a new office in Bangalore — what steps would you take?

---

## IMPLIED FOLLOW-UPS (FROM SAME SCENARIO)

13. What is the objective of the event?
14. How will you measure success?
15. Who will you invite?
16. What logistics will you plan (location, setup, timing)?
17. What stakeholders will you involve?

---

## META / INTERVIEWER EXPECTATION QUESTIONS (IMPLICIT)

18. How do you handle ambiguity?
19. How do you work in a team?
20. How do you help others succeed?
21. How do you push yourself outside your comfort zone?
22. How do you demonstrate leadership without a formal role?
