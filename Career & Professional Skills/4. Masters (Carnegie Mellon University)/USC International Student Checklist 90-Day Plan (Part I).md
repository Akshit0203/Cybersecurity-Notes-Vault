# Your 90 Day Checklist after arriving in the US

*   [ ] **Immigration Status Verification**
    *   Your very first to-do. USC's Office of International Services requires all new international students to complete ISV within the first few days of arriving. It is super simple and can be done online.

*   [ ] **Open a US Bank Account**
    *   Head to USC Credit Union, Chase or Bank of America on campus in your first week. From paying rent to splitting dinner with new friends, a US bank account is essential. Bring your passport, I-20, I-94 & university ID.

*   [ ] **Get a US SIM Card**
    *   Mint Mobile, T-Mobile, and Google Fi are popular with international students. A US number makes everything easier — from job applications to cab rides.

*   [ ] **Visit the Career Centre**
    *   The earlier you visit, the better. Get your resume reviewed, learn about upcoming career fairs, and find out when you can start applying for internships on your visa status.

*   [ ] **Update your LinkedIn & Start Networking**
    *   Add your USC Viterbi programme. Connect with classmates, professors, and alumni. The Trojan Network is one of the strongest in the world — start using it from day one.

*   [ ] **Join at least 2 Student Clubs**
    *   One technical, one social. This is how you build your network, find your people, and add real experience to your resume. USC has 900+ clubs. There's USC Racing, Trojan Cricket Club, Association of Indian Students, Magic Association. There's truly something for everyone.

*   [ ] **Learn about CPT, OPT & STEM**
    *   You won't need CPT/OPT/STEM for a while — but understanding it early means you won't miss critical deadlines later. Pop in to the Office of International Services in your first month to learn more about the process.

*   [ ] **Apply for your Social Security Number**
    *   If you have an on-campus job, an internship, or a job, applying for your SSN is mandatory — it means your career in the US is officially getting started. Apply as soon as you're eligible and keep it safe. You'll use it for years to come.

*   [ ] **Attend your First USC Career Fair**
    *   Go in with a shortlist of companies you love, an elevator pitch that sounds like you, and a well-curated resume. Follow up with recruiters on LinkedIn the same day — a personal message referencing what you discussed can turn conversations into opportunities.
