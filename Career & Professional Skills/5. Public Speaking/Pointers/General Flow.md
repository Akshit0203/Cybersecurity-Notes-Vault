# General Flow — Session Delivery Guide

---

## Main Mindset

- Just put your thoughts forward.
- Say: "I'm just putting my thoughts forward — not saying this is right or this is wrong, or that you will reach a certain point if you do this or that."

---

## Raise the Energy — But Stay Technical

- You have to raise the energy of the room.
- But at the same time, be technical as well.
- Don't let it happen that people say: "It's a lot of fun in his sessions, but I didn't learn anything."

---

## Just Show Up and Apply

- Most of the opportunities I got were not by thinking and preparing for an event — it was just showing up and applying.
- We tend to overthink: "When I'm ready, then I'll apply." But someone else just applies, shows up, and gets in.
- Don't wait until you're fully ready. Increase your visibility online.
- If you don't tell anyone what you do, how will offers come to you?
- Apply to as many opportunities as you can.
- There are two extremes to aim for:
    - "I will become a master of my work."
    - "Bring on whatever challenge or interview comes — I'm not afraid."

## LinkedIn & GitHub — Why It Matters

### Why Keep a Strong LinkedIn Profile?

- First, it's about talking about your passion.
- Making connections with people doing the same work — you can learn from those doing it better than you.
- I also used to think maintaining a LinkedIn profile wasn't that important — until I started getting recruiter messages and emails reaching out directly.
- Imagine a life where:
    - You didn't apply anywhere
    - You had no referral
    - But recruiters are reaching out to you on their own

## Think from a Skill Point of View

- Think always from a **skill** point of view — not a certification or a course.
- Mind mapping is the best way to plan your learning — show step by step with images.
- Certifications are not useless — but going after a certification spree is useless.

---

## Only Conduct Sessions You Learn From

- Only conduct sessions in which you will also learn something — sessions that move you forward in the direction you want to go.
- Make one common order flow for all seminars.

---

## My Internship Journey

1. First, I used to **pay** for internships — ₹200, ₹300 for a certificate
2. Then I **cold emailed** people — "I am doing this, I liked this, can you give me an internship?"
3. Then I started **winning all-India government internships**
4. Then I just **participated for fun**, sharing experience from previous internships
5. Now I'm even getting **full-time offers from FAANG/MAANG** companies

## The Hard Truth About Getting a Job

> If anyone ever feels like "Where are the jobs?" — the hard truth is: either you don't know the work, no matter how harsh that sounds. And if you do know the work, then you don't know how to communicate it or how to market yourself. Focus on these two things starting today — a path will open up somewhere.
