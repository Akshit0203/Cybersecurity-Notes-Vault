
# USSD Codes and Mobile Diagnostic Commands – Notes

## What Is a USSD Code?

USSD stands for:

**Unstructured Supplementary Service Data**

USSD is a protocol used by mobile networks that allows communication between a mobile device and the telecom operator's systems.

USSD codes usually start with:

Examples:

- *123#
    
- *121#
    
- *99#
    

Unlike SMS, USSD sessions are interactive and happen in real time.

---

# Common Mobile Codes

## `*#06#`

### Purpose

Displays the device's:

**IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity)**

### Example

Dial:

```text
*#06#
```

Result:

- IMEI 1
    
- IMEI 2 (Dual SIM devices)
    

---

## Why Is IMEI Important?

The IMEI uniquely identifies a mobile device.

Uses:

- Reporting stolen phones
    
- Device tracking investigations
    
- CEIR/Sanchar Saathi registration
    
- Telecom identification
    

Example:

If a phone is stolen, investigators often ask for:

- IMEI number
    
- Mobile number
    
- Device model
    

---

# `*99#`

### Purpose

Accesses:

**USSD-Based Mobile Banking**

NPCI's National Unified USSD Platform (NUUP)

### Example

Dial:

```text
*99#
```

Features:

- Balance inquiry
    
- Fund transfer
    
- Mini statement
    
- UPI-related services (supported networks)
    

---

## Why Was `*99#` Created?

Many users:

- Did not have smartphones
    
- Did not have internet access
    
- Lived in rural areas
    

`*99#` allows banking through basic GSM phones.

---

# Difference Between USSD and SMS

|USSD|SMS|
|---|---|
|Real-time communication|Store-and-forward|
|Session based|Message based|
|Usually starts with * and ends with #|Standard text message|
|Immediate response|May be delayed|
|Used for banking and telecom services|Used for messaging|

---

# Important Note

Many websites claim "secret mobile codes."

Be careful:

- Codes vary by manufacturer.
    
- Some diagnostic codes can modify settings.
    
- Some codes may trigger factory resets on specific devices.
    

Always verify before using unknown codes.

---

# Key Takeaway

USSD codes provide direct interaction between a mobile device and telecom or banking systems. One of the most important codes is:

```text
*#06#
```

which displays the device IMEI, while:

```text
*99#
```

is used in India for USSD-based banking services through the National Unified USSD Platform.
