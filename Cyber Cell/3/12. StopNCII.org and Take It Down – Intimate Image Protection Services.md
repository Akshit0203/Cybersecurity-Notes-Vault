
# StopNCII.org and Take It Down – Intimate Image Protection Services

## Overview

Two important global initiatives help victims prevent the spread of intimate images online:

### 1. StopNCII.org

For:

- Adults (18+)
    

Purpose:

- Prevent the sharing of non-consensual intimate images online.
    

---

### 2. Take It Down

For:

- Images or videos taken when the person was under 18 years old.
    

Purpose:

- Prevent or remove the online sharing of child sexual abuse material (CSAM) and self-generated intimate images involving minors.
    

---

# 1. StopNCII.org

Source:

## What Is It?

StopNCII.org (Stop Non-Consensual Intimate Image Abuse) is a free service operated by the UK-based Revenge Porn Helpline (RPH), part of SWGfL.

It helps victims prevent intimate images from being uploaded and shared on participating platforms.

---

## Who Can Use It?

You can use StopNCII.org if:

- You are the person in the image.
    
- You were 18 or older when the image was taken.
    
- You are currently over 18.
    
- You still possess the image/video.
    
- The image contains nudity, semi-nudity, or sexual activity.
    

---

## How It Works

### Step 1

User selects the intimate image/video on their device.

### Step 2

The image never leaves the device.

### Step 3

A cryptographic hash is generated.

Hash = Digital Fingerprint

### Step 4

Only the hash is uploaded.

### Step 5

Participating companies receive the hash.

### Step 6

Platforms compare uploaded content against known hashes.

### Step 7

Matching content can be blocked or removed.

---

## Important Privacy Feature

The actual image:

❌ Is not uploaded

❌ Is not viewed by staff

❌ Is not stored on StopNCII servers

Only:

✅ Hash value (digital fingerprint)

is shared.

---

## Success Rate

According to StopNCII:

- Over 90% removal rate
    
- More than 300,000 individual intimate images removed
    

---

# What Is NCII?

NCII stands for:

**Non-Consensual Intimate Images**

Examples:

- Revenge porn
    
- Intimate image abuse
    
- Threats to leak intimate content
    
- Sharing private images without consent
    

---

# 2. Take It Down

## What Is It?

Take It Down is operated by:

**National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC)**

It is designed for images/videos taken when the victim was under 18 years old.

---

## Who Is It For?

People who:

- Were under 18 when the image/video was created.
    
- Believe the image has been shared or may be shared online.
    
- Want to prevent further distribution.
    

Even if the victim is now an adult, the image qualifies if it was created before age 18.

---

## How It Works

### Step 1

Select image/video on device.

### Step 2

Hash generated locally.

### Step 3

Image never leaves device.

### Step 4

Hash sent to NCMEC.

### Step 5

Participating platforms receive hash database.

### Step 6

Platforms scan public or unencrypted content.

### Step 7

Matching content may be removed or blocked.

---

## Privacy Protection

Take It Down specifically states:

- Do NOT upload the image.
    
- Do NOT send the image.
    
- Do NOT download or redistribute the image.
    

Only the hash is submitted.

---

# Hashing Explained

Hashing creates:

Image

↓

Algorithm

↓

Unique Hash Value

Example:

```text
IMG001.jpg
↓
7A4B92D81F...
```

Every identical copy of the image produces the same hash.

This allows platforms to identify the image without seeing the image itself.

# Difference Between StopNCII and Take It Down

|StopNCII|Take It Down|
|---|---|
|Adults (18+)|Images taken before age 18|
|Run by Revenge Porn Helpline (UK)|Run by NCMEC (USA)|
|NCII / Revenge Porn|Child sexual abuse & youth intimate images|
|Hash-based protection|Hash-based protection|
|Image never uploaded|Image never uploaded|

# Key Takeaway

StopNCII.org and Take It Down are privacy-preserving services that use image hashing technology to help prevent the spread of intimate images online. The actual image never leaves the victim's device; only a digital fingerprint (hash) is shared with participating platforms. StopNCII is intended for adults (18+), while Take It Down is intended for images or videos created when the victim was under 18 years old.
