
# INTERPOL vs Indian Cyber Cells – Key Differences

## Common Misconception

Many people think INTERPOL is an international police force that can directly arrest criminals anywhere in the world.

This is incorrect.

INTERPOL is primarily an international coordination organization.

Actual arrests are made by national law enforcement agencies.

---

# What Is INTERPOL?

INTERPOL stands for:

**International Criminal Police Organization**

Headquarters:

Lyon

Members:

- More than 190 countries
    

Purpose:

- Facilitate international police cooperation
    
- Share intelligence
    
- Assist cross-border investigations
    
- Coordinate international operations
    

---

# What Is an Indian Cyber Cell?

Cyber Cells are law enforcement units operating within India.

Examples:

- State Cyber Crime Police Stations
    
- Special Cyber Cells
    
- Central agencies investigating cybercrime
    

Purpose:

- Investigate cybercrime within Indian jurisdiction
    
- Register FIRs
    
- Seize devices
    
- Make arrests
    
- Collect digital evidence
    

---

# Biggest Difference

## Indian Cyber Cell

Can:

✅ Register FIR

✅ Search premises

✅ Seize devices

✅ Arrest suspects

✅ File chargesheets

✅ Conduct forensic examinations

---

## INTERPOL

Cannot:

❌ Register FIR in India

❌ Arrest suspects

❌ Conduct raids in India

❌ Seize devices directly

❌ Force countries to take action

Instead, INTERPOL helps countries work together.

---

# Example Scenario

Suppose:

A cybercriminal in:

Russia

targets victims in:

India

An Indian Cyber Cell can:

- Investigate victims
    
- Analyze malware
    
- Collect evidence
    

But cannot simply travel to Russia and arrest the suspect.

At this point:

- International cooperation becomes necessary.
    
- INTERPOL may assist with information sharing.
    

---

# INTERPOL Notices

INTERPOL is famous for issuing notices.

### Red Notice

Most famous notice.

Purpose:

- Request member countries to locate and provisionally detain a wanted person.
    

Important:

A Red Notice is **not an international arrest warrant**.

Each country decides what action to take.

---

### Blue Notice

Purpose:

- Collect additional information about a person.
    

---

### Yellow Notice

Purpose:

- Missing persons.
    
- Identification of unknown persons.
    

---

### Black Notice

Purpose:

- Identification of unidentified deceased persons.

# Investigation Lesson

A local cybercrime usually starts with:

- Cyber Cell
    
- State Police
    
- Central Agency
    

When the crime crosses borders:

- Mutual Legal Assistance
    
- International cooperation
    
- INTERPOL coordination
    

become necessary.

---

# Key Takeaway

Indian Cyber Cells are operational law enforcement units that can investigate, search, seize, and arrest within their jurisdiction.

INTERPOL is not a global police force. Its primary role is to connect law enforcement agencies across countries, facilitate intelligence sharing, coordinate investigations, and help track international criminals when cybercrime crosses national borders.
