# 1. Overview

India has a coordinated mechanism to respond to financial cyber frauds through:

- **National Cyber Crime Helpline (1930)**
    
- **Citizen Financial Cyber Fraud Reporting and Management System (CFCFRMS)**
    
- Banks and payment service providers
    
- Fintech companies
    
- Law enforcement agencies
    
- Merchant platforms and service providers
    

The primary objective is to **freeze or stop fraudulent transactions as quickly as possible** before the money is withdrawn or further transferred.

# 3. Role of the 1930 Helpline

The **1930 helpline** is the national helpline for reporting financial cyber frauds.

When a victim calls:

- A call center agent records the complaint.
    
- Important details such as transaction ID, account number, UPI ID, amount, and time are collected.
    
- A case is generated and escalated through the financial fraud response system.
    

---

# 4. Ticket Escalation Process

After the complaint is registered:

1. The reported transaction is examined.
    
2. The relevant bank or payment institution is notified.
    
3. If the funds are still available, appropriate action may be taken to prevent further movement in accordance with applicable procedures.
    
4. If the money has already moved to another institution, the alert may be propagated to the next identified institution in the transaction chain.
    

This process continues until the trail is exhausted or the funds are located.

---

# 5. Lien Marking (Hold on Funds)

A **lien mark** is an operational mechanism used by banks to restrict the movement or withdrawal of identified funds while the matter is examined.

Example:

```text
Victim
   │
   ▼
Fraudster Account
   │
   ▼
₹50,000 Received
   │
   ▼
Bank Applies Lien / Hold
   │
   ▼
Funds Temporarily Restricted
```

Whether and how a hold is applied depends on the facts, bank procedures, and legal requirements.

# 9. ATM and Cheque Withdrawals

Fraudsters may attempt to cash out using:

- ATM withdrawals
    
- Cheque withdrawals
    
- Branch withdrawals
    
- Transfers to additional accounts
    

Rapid reporting increases the possibility of intervention before funds are withdrawn.

- Emails

Prompt reporting improves the chances of tracing funds.

---

# 12. Key Takeaways

- Financial cyber frauds in India can be reported through the **1930 helpline** or the official cybercrime reporting mechanisms.
    
- Complaints are recorded, assessed, and escalated to relevant banks or payment institutions.
    
- If suspicious funds are identified in time, banks may place operational restrictions such as a **lien/hold** to help prevent further movement.
    
- If funds have already moved, alerts can be propagated across the transaction chain to other participating institutions.
    
- A broad ecosystem of banks, fintechs, payment providers, merchants, and in some cases logistics partners supports rapid fraud response and evidence preservation.
    
- **Speed is critical**: the sooner a fraud is reported, the greater the possibility of interrupting the financial kill chain and improving recovery prospects.
