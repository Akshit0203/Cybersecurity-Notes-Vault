
# AEPS Fraud (Aadhaar Enabled Payment System Fraud) – Investigation Notes

## What Is AEPS?

AEPS stands for:

**Aadhaar Enabled Payment System**

It is a banking system developed by the National Payments Corporation of India (NPCI) that allows banking transactions using:

- Aadhaar Number
    
- Bank Name
    
- Fingerprint Authentication
    

A customer does not need:

- Debit card
    
- Credit card
    
- Mobile banking
    

Fingerprint authentication is sufficient.

---

## How AEPS Works

### Normal Transaction Flow

Customer

↓

Provides Aadhaar Number

↓

Selects Bank

↓

Places Finger on Biometric Scanner

↓

Fingerprint Verified

↓

Transaction Processed

↓

Cash Withdrawal / Balance Inquiry

---

## Why AEPS Became a Target

Attackers realized that if they could obtain:

- Aadhaar Number
    
- Biometric Data (Fingerprint)
    

they could potentially perform unauthorized transactions.

---

# Common AEPS Fraud Methods

## 1. Biometric Cloning Fraud

Criminal obtains:

- Aadhaar number
    
- Fingerprint image
    

The fingerprint may be collected from:

- Documents
    
- Glass surfaces
    
- Fake enrollment activities
    
- Malicious biometric collection
    

The attacker creates a fake fingerprint mold.

The cloned fingerprint is used for AEPS transactions.

---

## 2. Fake KYC Camps

Fraudsters conduct:

- Fake government camps
    
- Fake subsidy registration drives
    
- Fake bank verification drives
    

Victims provide:

- Aadhaar details
    
- Biometric information
    

The data is later abused.

---

## 3. Insider Fraud

A rogue operator may misuse:

- Customer information
    
- Biometric devices
    
- Banking access
    

This has occurred in some AEPS fraud investigations.

---

## 4. Social Engineering Fraud

Fraudsters convince victims to provide:

- Aadhaar copies
    
- OTPs
    
- Biometric scans
    

under false pretenses.

---

# Typical AEPS Fraud Investigation

## Stage 1 – Victim Complaint

Victim reports:

> "Money was withdrawn but I never visited any AEPS center."

---

## Stage 2 – Transaction Analysis

Investigators collect:

- Transaction logs
    
- Aadhaar details
    
- Device information
    
- Banking records
    

---

## Stage 3 – Agent Identification

Each AEPS transaction is linked to:

- Agent ID
    
- Merchant ID
    
- Device ID
    

Investigators identify:

- Which AEPS terminal was used
    
- Which retailer initiated the transaction
    

---

## Stage 4 – CCTV Verification

Investigators obtain:

- CCTV footage
    
- Shop recordings
    
- Witness statements
    

to determine who performed the transaction.

---

## Stage 5 – Biometric Examination

Investigators determine:

- Whether genuine fingerprints were used
    
- Whether cloned fingerprints were used
    
- Whether biometric devices were tampered with
    

---

## Stage 6 – Money Trail Analysis

The most important step:

Follow the money.

Investigators track:

Victim Account

↓

Merchant Account

↓

Withdrawal Point

↓

Beneficiary

This often identifies the fraud network.

---

# Digital Evidence Used

### Banking Logs

Reveal:

- Transaction times
    
- Amounts
    
- Agent IDs
    

---

### AEPS Device Logs

Reveal:

- Terminal information
    
- Device usage history
    

---

### CCTV Footage

Reveal:

- Physical operators
    
- Cash withdrawals
    

---

### Telecom Records

Reveal:

- Communication between suspects
    
- Location correlation
    

---

### Aadhaar Authentication Logs

May reveal:

- Authentication attempts
    
- Transaction metadata
    

---

# Red Flags

Signs of possible AEPS fraud:

- Unexplained withdrawals
    
- SMS alerts for unknown transactions
    
- Frequent biometric authentication failures
    
- Requests for fingerprints by unknown individuals
    
- Unauthorized Aadhaar collection
    

---

# Prevention

### Lock Biometrics

UIDAI provides biometric lock functionality.

When enabled:

- Biometric authentication becomes disabled.
    
- Fraud risk is reduced.
    

---

### Avoid Sharing Aadhaar Copies

Only share when necessary.

---

### Verify Operators

Use trusted:

- Banks
    
- CSC centers
    
- Authorized service providers
    

---

### Monitor SMS Alerts

Check banking alerts immediately.

---

# Key Takeaway

AEPS fraud typically involves misuse of Aadhaar-linked banking services through stolen, cloned, or fraudulently collected biometric information. Most investigations focus on transaction logs, biometric authentication records, merchant IDs, CCTV footage, and money-trail analysis to identify the individuals responsible for unauthorized withdrawals.
