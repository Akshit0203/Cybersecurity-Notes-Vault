
# Phone Stolen – Immediate Response Checklist

## Goal

When a phone is stolen, your priorities should be:

1. Protect your SIM card
    
2. Protect your bank accounts
    
3. Protect your digital identity
    
4. Block the device
    
5. Attempt recovery
    

The first few hours are critical.

---

# Step 1 – Use Find My Device / Find My iPhone

Immediately attempt to locate the phone.

### Android

Use:

- Google Find My Device
    

### Samsung

Use:

- SmartThings Find / Find My Mobile
    

### Apple

Use:

- Find My
    

Possible actions:

- View location
    
- Play sound
    
- Mark as lost
    
- Lock device
    
- Erase data remotely
    

---

# Step 2 – Remote Lock the Device

If recovery is uncertain:

Enable:

- Lost Mode (iPhone)
    
- Secure Device (Android)
    

This prevents unauthorized access.

Benefits:

- Locks the screen
    
- Displays contact information
    
- Prevents normal use
    

---

# Step 3 – Check Last Known Location

Even if the thief powers off the phone:

You may still obtain:

- Last known location
    
- Last connected network
    
- Last active timestamp
    

This information can assist police investigations.

---

# Step 4 – Block the SIM Card

Contact your telecom operator immediately.

Request:

- SIM suspension
    
- SIM block
    

This prevents:

- OTP theft
    
- Banking fraud
    
- WhatsApp takeover
    
- Account recovery abuse
    

---

# Step 5 – Enable SIM PIN Protection (Future Prevention)

Before theft occurs, enable:

SIM PIN Lock

Benefits:

- SIM cannot be used after removal
    
- PIN required after reboot
    
- Reduces unauthorized access
    

---

# Step 6 – Freeze Banking Access

Immediately contact:

- Bank
    
- Credit card issuer
    
- UPI provider
    

Request:

- Debit freeze
    
- Temporary transaction block
    

Protect:

- UPI
    
- Mobile banking
    
- Wallets
    
- Linked payment apps
    

---

# Step 7 – Block UPI and Wallet Services

Check:

- Google Pay
    
- PhonePe
    
- Paytm
    
- Banking applications
    

Log out remotely where possible.

Change:

- UPI PIN
    
- Banking passwords
    

---

# Step 8 – Reissue SIM Card

Visit:

- Authorized telecom store
    

Request:

- SIM replacement
    
- SIM reissue
    

Keep:

- Same mobile number
    
- New SIM card
    

This restores access to:

- OTPs
    
- Banking
    
- Recovery accounts
    

---

# Step 9 – Block Device Through Sanchar Saathi

Use:

**[https://sancharsaathi.gov.in](https://sancharsaathi.gov.in/)**

CEIR Services:

- Report stolen phone
    
- Block IMEI
    
- Register complaint
    
- Request recovery assistance
    

Benefits:

- Device becomes difficult to use on Indian telecom networks.
    
- Future usage may generate investigative leads.
    

---

# Step 10 – Change Critical Passwords

Change passwords for:

- Gmail
    
- Apple ID
    
- Microsoft Account
    
- Banking apps
    
- Social media
    
- Cloud storage
    

Prioritize accounts linked to:

- OTP recovery
    
- Financial services
    

---

# Step 11 – Monitor Account Activity

Review:

- Login history
    
- Banking transactions
    
- UPI records
    
- Email activity
    

Watch for:

- Unknown logins
    
- Password reset attempts
    
- Unauthorized transactions
    

---

# Step 12 – File a Police Complaint

Provide:

- IMEI number
    
- Phone number
    
- Device model
    
- Last known location
    
- Theft details
    

Keep copies of:

- FIR
    
- Complaint number
    
- IMEI records
    

---

# Find My Device and Offline Tracking

Modern devices may support:

### Android Find Hub / Find My Device Network

Can help locate devices using nearby Android devices.

### Apple Find My Network

Can locate devices using nearby Apple devices even when the phone is offline or powered off (on supported models with remaining battery).

### Samsung SmartThings Find

Uses nearby Samsung devices to assist in locating lost devices.

---

# Emergency Priority Order

1. Locate phone
    
2. Remote lock device
    
3. Block SIM
    
4. Freeze banking access
    
5. Change passwords
    
6. Reissue SIM
    
7. Block IMEI via Sanchar Saathi
    
8. File police complaint
    
9. Monitor accounts
    

---

# Key Takeaway

A stolen phone is not just a lost device—it is a potential identity theft and financial fraud risk. The fastest way to reduce damage is to immediately lock the device, block the SIM, secure banking accounts, reissue the SIM, report the IMEI through Sanchar Saathi, and use Find My Device or Find My iPhone to assist recovery efforts.
