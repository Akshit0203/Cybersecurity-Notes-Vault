
# Quishing, Drive-by Downloads and Reverse UPI Request Fraud

## 1. Quishing (QR Code Phishing)

### What Is Quishing?

Quishing =

**QR Code + Phishing**

Instead of sending a malicious link, attackers send a malicious QR code.

When scanned, the QR code may:

- Open a phishing website
    
- Trigger a payment request
    
- Download malware
    
- Redirect to fake login pages
    

---

## Why Is Quishing Effective?

Users cannot easily see the destination URL before scanning.

People often trust QR codes found on:

- Posters
    
- Restaurant tables
    
- Parking meters
    
- WhatsApp messages
    
- Emails
    
- Social media posts
    

---

## Example Attack

Victim receives:

"Pay electricity bill"

↓

QR Code

↓

Scans QR

↓

Fake website opens

↓

Enters credentials

↓

Account compromised

---

## Investigation Evidence

Investigators analyze:

- QR code contents
    
- Destination URL
    
- Domain registration
    
- Hosting provider
    
- IP logs
    
- Payment trail
    

---

# 2. Drive-By Download

### What Is a Drive-By Download?

A drive-by download occurs when visiting a malicious or compromised website causes unwanted software to be downloaded or installed.

In some cases:

- No download button is clicked.
    
- Exploitation occurs automatically through browser vulnerabilities.
    

---

## Attack Flow

Victim Visits Website

↓

Malicious Script Executes

↓

Browser Vulnerability Triggered

↓

Malware Downloaded

↓

System Compromised

---

## Common Payloads

- Trojans
    
- Keyloggers
    
- Spyware
    
- Banking malware
    
- Ransomware
    

---

## Investigation Evidence

Investigators examine:

- Browser history
    
- DNS records
    
- Download logs
    
- Antivirus logs
    
- Malware samples
    
- Network traffic
    

---

## Prevention

- Keep browser updated
    
- Keep operating system patched
    
- Use reputable antivirus
    
- Avoid suspicious websites
    

---

# 3. Reverse UPI Request Fraud

### Common Scam

Scammer says:

> "I am sending you money."

Victim receives:

UPI Collect Request

Instead of receiving money, the victim is actually being asked to approve a payment.

---

## How UPI Works

### Receive Money

No PIN required.

Money comes into account.

---

### Send Money

UPI PIN required.

Money leaves account.

---

## Scam Flow

Scammer:

"I'll pay you ₹10,000."

↓

Victim receives Collect Request

↓

Victim enters UPI PIN

↓

Money transferred to scammer

---

## Important Rule

### Receiving Money

❌ Never requires UPI PIN

### Sending Money

✅ Requires UPI PIN

---

## QR Code Payment Scam

Scammer sends:

QR Code

Claim:

> "Scan this QR to receive money."

Reality:

QR opens payment request.

↓

Victim scans

↓

Payment page opens

↓

Victim enters PIN

↓

Money debited

---

## Investigation Evidence

Investigators examine:

- UPI transaction ID
    
- UTR number
    
- Bank records
    
- Beneficiary account
    
- Device logs
    
- Mobile number
    
- IP records
    

---

# Red Flags

### Quishing

- Unknown QR codes
    
- QR codes pasted over original ones
    
- QR codes received from strangers
    

---

### Drive-By Download

- Sudden software installation
    
- Browser redirects
    
- Unexpected downloads
    

---

### Reverse UPI Fraud

- Asked to scan QR to receive money
    
- Asked to enter PIN to receive money
    
- Unexpected collect requests
    

---

# Key Takeaway

### Quishing

Uses malicious QR codes to redirect victims to phishing sites, malware, or fraudulent payment pages.

### Drive-By Download

Malware is downloaded or installed simply by visiting a malicious or compromised website, often through browser vulnerabilities.

### Reverse UPI Request Fraud

Scammers trick victims into approving a payment request. Remember: **You never need to enter a UPI PIN to receive money. Entering a PIN means money is being sent, not received.**
