# Police Cyber Cell vs Military Cyber Roles

Many people assume that all cybersecurity jobs involve:

- Bug bounty
    
- VAPT
    
- Web hacking
    
- Red teaming
    

However, the work done in a Police Cyber Cell is very different from the work done in military cyber units.

---

# Police Cyber Cell

The primary objective of a Police Cyber Cell is:

- Investigating cybercrime
    
- Identifying suspects
    
- Collecting evidence
    
- Supporting legal prosecution
    

The focus is on finding **who committed the crime** and proving it through evidence.

---

## Common Areas of Work

### Cybercrime Investigation

Activities:

- CDR analysis
    
- IPDR analysis
    
- Tower dump analysis
    
- Telecom investigations
    
- Social media investigations
    
- Email tracing
    

Goal:

Identify suspects and establish links between digital activities and individuals.

---

### Digital Forensics

Activities:

- Mobile forensics
    
- Computer forensics
    
- Memory analysis
    
- Cloud forensics
    
- Data recovery
    

Goal:

Recover and analyze digital evidence.

---

### Financial Fraud Investigation

Activities:

- Banking fraud analysis
    
- UPI fraud investigations
    
- Cryptocurrency tracing
    
- Money trail analysis
    

Goal:

Track stolen funds and identify beneficiaries.

---

### OSINT and Attribution

Activities:

- Open-source intelligence gathering
    
- Social media analysis
    
- Dark web monitoring
    
- Identity correlation
    

Goal:

Attribute cyber activities to real individuals or groups.

---

## Skills Commonly Developed

- Digital evidence handling
    
- Legal procedures
    
- Court admissibility
    
- Telecom analysis
    
- OSINT
    
- Attribution techniques
    
- Investigation methodology
    

---

# Military Cyber Roles

The primary objective of military cyber units is:

- National security
    
- Cyber defence
    
- Strategic intelligence
    
- Protection of military infrastructure
    

The focus is on defending the country against cyber threats and supporting military operations.

---

## Common Areas of Work

### Cyber Defence

Activities:

- Protecting military networks
    
- Securing communication systems
    
- Monitoring critical infrastructure
    
- Defensive security operations
    

Goal:

Prevent disruption of military capabilities.

---

### Cyber Intelligence

Activities:

- Tracking nation-state threats
    
- Monitoring foreign cyber operations
    
- Strategic threat assessment
    
- Intelligence collection
    

Goal:

Understand adversaries and anticipate threats.

---

### Malware Analysis

Activities:

- Reverse engineering malware
    
- Analyzing espionage tools
    
- Studying APT campaigns
    
- Threat intelligence research
    

Goal:

Understand attacker capabilities and techniques.

---

### Threat Hunting

Activities:

- Detecting advanced intrusions
    
- Identifying hidden threats
    
- Monitoring suspicious activity
    
- Investigating indicators of compromise
    

Goal:

Find threats before they cause damage.

---

### Security Assessment

Activities:

- Vulnerability assessments
    
- Infrastructure security reviews
    
- Security audits
    
- Risk assessments
    

Goal:

Strengthen military cyber resilience.

---

# Key Difference

|Police Cyber Cell|Military Cyber Units|
|---|---|
|Investigates cybercrime|Defends national infrastructure|
|Focuses on criminals and suspects|Focuses on adversaries and nation-state threats|
|Collects evidence for court|Collects intelligence for defence operations|
|Handles fraud, scams, and cybercrime cases|Handles cyber warfare and strategic threats|
|Uses forensics and attribution extensively|Uses intelligence, defence, and threat analysis extensively|
|Goal is prosecution|Goal is national security|

---

# Which One Teaches What?

### Police Cyber Cell

You are more likely to learn:

- CDR/IPDR analysis
    
- Telecom investigations
    
- Mobile forensics
    
- Financial fraud investigations
    
- Cryptocurrency tracing
    
- Digital evidence handling
    
- Legal and procedural aspects of cybercrime
    

---

### Military Cyber Roles

You are more likely to learn:

- Cyber defence operations
    
- Threat intelligence
    
- Malware analysis
    
- Threat hunting
    
- Strategic cyber security
    
- Protection of critical infrastructure
    
- Nation-state threat analysis
    

---

# Key Takeaway

A Police Cyber Cell is primarily focused on cybercrime investigation, digital forensics, attribution, and legal evidence collection. Military cyber units are focused on cyber defence, intelligence gathering, threat analysis, and protecting national security systems. While both operate in cybersecurity, their objectives, skills, and day-to-day work are significantly different.
