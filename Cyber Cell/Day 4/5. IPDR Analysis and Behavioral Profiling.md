## What Can IPDR Reveal?

IPDR (Internet Protocol Detail Records) can help investigators reconstruct a person's digital behavior patterns by analyzing internet activity over time.

IPDR may reveal:

- Internet session timings
    
- Assigned IP addresses
    
- Application usage patterns
    
- Data consumption
    
- Connection durations
    
- Device activity timelines
    

The exact level of detail depends on the telecom operator, service provider, available logs, and legal authorities involved.

---

## How IPDR Detects Abnormal Behavior

IPDR itself does not directly label behavior as abnormal. Instead, investigators first establish a person's normal usage pattern (baseline behavior) and then look for significant deviations from that pattern.

Examples of abnormal behavior may include:

- Internet activity during hours when the user is normally inactive
    
- Sudden increases or decreases in data usage
    
- Unusual application usage
    
- Frequent connections to new services or platforms
    
- Changes in communication frequency
    
- Unexpected changes in location combined with internet activity
    

The more historical data available, the easier it becomes to identify unusual patterns.

---

## Sleep Pattern Analysis

By examining internet activity over multiple days, investigators may estimate:

- Typical bedtime
    
- Wake-up time
    
- Active hours
    
- Inactive periods
    

Example:

- Last activity every night around 1:30 AM
    
- First activity every morning around 8:00 AM
    

This may indicate a regular sleep window between those times.

### Detecting Abnormal Sleep-Related Activity

If a user normally becomes inactive at 1:30 AM but suddenly shows heavy internet usage between 3:00 AM and 5:00 AM for several days, investigators may consider this a behavioral change worth examining.

Such changes do not automatically indicate wrongdoing but may be relevant during an investigation.

## Day vs Night Activity Analysis

IPDR can help determine:

### Daytime Usage

- Work-related activity
    
- Social media access
    
- Web browsing
    
- Navigation applications
    

### Night-Time Usage

- Streaming services
    
- Gaming
    
- Social networking
    
- Messaging activity
    

Investigators often compare behavioral changes before and after significant events.

### Detecting Abnormal Usage Patterns

Examples include:

- A user who normally streams videos at night suddenly stops all activity.
    
- A user who rarely uses messaging applications suddenly generates extensive messaging-related traffic.
    
- Significant shifts in active hours over a short period.
    

These deviations may indicate changes in routine or behavior.

---

## Late-Night Activity Detection

Unusual activity may be identified such as:

- Device active at 3:00 AM
    
- Multiple internet sessions during sleeping hours
    
- Sudden spikes in communication
    

These patterns may become relevant during timeline reconstruction.

### Example

Normal pattern:

- Device inactive from 2:00 AM to 8:00 AM
    

Abnormal pattern:

- Device active continuously between 2:00 AM and 5:00 AM
    
- Multiple messaging sessions during that period
    

This deviation may warrant further analysis.

## Communication Pattern Analysis

Even when message content is unavailable, investigators may identify:

- When communication occurred
    
- Frequency of communication
    
- Duration of internet sessions
    
- Correlation between multiple devices
    

For example:

- Device A shows WhatsApp activity from 9:00–9:05 AM.
    
- Device B shows matching WhatsApp activity from 9:00–9:05 AM.
    

This may suggest a communication event occurred between the two parties.

### Detecting Communication Anomalies

Investigators may look for:

- Sudden increases in communication frequency
    
- Communication at unusual times
    
- New recurring communication patterns
    
- Abrupt cessation of regular communication
    

Example:

- A user normally communicates with a contact once per week.
    
- Activity suddenly increases to dozens of sessions per day.
    

This change may be considered abnormal relative to the user's historical behavior.

## Application Usage Indicators

IPDR may sometimes indicate connections to services such as:

- WhatsApp
    
- Telegram
    
- Discord
    
- Instagram
    
- Facebook
    
- YouTube
    
- Netflix
    
- Online gaming platforms
    

Important:

IPDR usually does not reveal message content, call recordings, or exact chats. It primarily shows metadata and network activity.

### Detecting Unusual Application Usage

Examples include:

- First-time use of a previously unused application
    
- Sudden migration from one messaging platform to another
    
- Large increases in usage duration
    
- Significant increases in data consumption associated with a specific service
    

Investigators compare these activities against historical usage patterns.

---

## Gaming Pattern Analysis

Investigators may identify:

- Regular gaming hours
    
- Online gaming sessions
    
- Duration of gameplay
    
- Frequency of game-server connections
    

Example:

- Daily connections to gaming servers between 10 PM and 1 AM.
    

This can help establish routine behavior.

### Detecting Gaming Anomalies

Examples:

- A user who normally plays for one hour suddenly spends eight hours online.
    
- Gaming activity appears during work hours when it was previously absent.
    
- Complete disappearance of a long-established gaming routine.
    

Such changes may indicate a shift in behavior.

## Timeline Reconstruction

One of the most powerful uses of IPDR is reconstructing events.

Example:

08:45 AM – Device active

09:00 AM – WhatsApp connection observed

09:05 AM – WhatsApp session ends

09:10 AM – Device location changes

09:15 AM – Multiple calls initiated

This helps investigators understand the sequence of actions surrounding an event.

### Identifying Abnormal Events in a Timeline

Investigators often compare a specific day's activity against historical records.

Example:

Normal pattern:

- Device active at home until 8:00 AM
    
- Work-related activity from 9:00 AM onward
    

Abnormal pattern:

- Device active throughout the night
    
- Unusual messaging activity before dawn
    
- Unexpected location change early in the morning
    

These deviations may become important during event reconstruction.

---

## Key Takeaway

IPDR is less about reading what someone did and more about understanding when, where, and how they used the internet.

Abnormal behavior is typically detected by comparing current activity against established historical patterns.

IPDR can help identify:

- Sleep pattern changes
    
- Daily routine deviations
    
- Unusual communication activity
    
- Unexpected application usage
    
- Sudden changes in data consumption
    
- Behavioral changes before and after an incident
    

Modern investigations often use IPDR analysis together with CDR analysis, device forensics, OSINT, and cloud data to build a complete picture of user activity.
