# Defence Cyber Agency (DCyA)

> **Type:** Integrated Tri-Services Agency — Indian Armed Forces
> **Established:** 28 September 2018 | **Operational:** August 2021
> **Headquarters:** New Delhi, India
> **Reports to:** Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) via Integrated Defence Staff (IDS)

---

## What It Does

The Defence Cyber Agency is India's **tri-service military agency for cyber warfare**. Its responsibilities include:

- **Cyber security** of Indian Armed Forces networks and systems
- **Offensive cyber operations** — hacking adversary networks, surveillance, deploying honeypots
- **Defensive cyber operations** — securing military networks, eliminating foreign hardware/software from armed forces systems
- **Digital forensics** — recovering deleted data from hard drives and cellphones
- **Cryptanalysis** — breaking into encrypted communication channels
- **Cyber warfare doctrine** — framing long-term policy for military cyber security
- **Cyber Emergency Response** — Army, Air Force, and Navy each operate their own CERT under DCyA coordination

In August 2025, the **"Joint Doctrine for Cyberspace Operations"** was released, aimed at defending national cyberspace through integrated offensive-defensive capabilities, real-time intelligence, and joint cyber force development.

## History

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **July 2011** | A national security task force was set up to review Kargil Review Committee recommendations |
| **May 2012** | Task Force submitted classified report recommending creation of a cyber command, aerospace command, and special operations command |
| **28 Sep 2018** | Creation of DCyA, Defence Space Agency, and Armed Forces Special Operations Division approved at Combined Commanders' Conference (Jodhpur) |
| **May 2019** | First head of DCyA appointed (Indian Navy Rear Admiral) |
| **Nov 2019** | Expected to become operational |
| **Aug 2021** | Fully operational — Army, Air Force, and Navy CERTs established |
| **7 Aug 2025** | Joint Doctrine for Cyberspace Operations released |

---

## Organisational Structure

- **Parent:** Integrated Defence Staff (IDS)
- **Personnel:** Drawn from all three branches — Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force
- **Head:** Two-star rank officer (Rear Admiral / Major General / Air Vice Marshal)
- **Predecessor:** Defence Information Assurance and Research Agency (upgraded to form DCyA)

## Related Organisations

- **Defence Space Agency (DSA)** — tri-service space warfare agency
- **Armed Forces Special Operations Division (AFSOD)** — tri-service special operations
- **Strategic Forces Command** — India's nuclear command
- **Integrated Defence Staff** — tri-service strategic command
