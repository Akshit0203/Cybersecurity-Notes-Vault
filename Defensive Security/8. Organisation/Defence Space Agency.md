# Defence Space Agency (DSA)

> **Type:** Integrated Tri-Services Agency — Indian Armed Forces
> **Established:** 28 September 2018 | **Operational:** November 2019
> **Headquarters:** Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
> **Reports to:** Chief of Defence Staff (CDS) via Integrated Defence Staff (IDS)

---

## What It Does

The Defence Space Agency is India's **tri-service military agency for space warfare and satellite intelligence**. Its core responsibilities include:

- **Space warfare strategy** — developing and implementing India's military space doctrine
- **Satellite intelligence (GEOINT)** — operating India's military and dual-use satellite constellation
- **Space-based surveillance** — leveraging SIGINT, ELINT, COMINT, and space-based tracking systems
- **Space asset protection** — defending Indian satellites from hostile electronic and physical attacks
- **Anti-satellite (ASAT) capabilities** — overseeing India's ASAT weapons programme (directed energy, co-orbital, laser, and EMP-based systems)
- **Military exercises** — conducting space warfare simulations (IndSpaceEx 2019, Antariksha Abhyas 2024)

In September 2025, the **"Joint Military Space Doctrine"** was released at the Combined Commanders' Conference in Kolkata.

The agency is expected to be upgraded into a **full-sized tri-service Space Command** in the future.

## Organisational Structure

- **Parent:** Integrated Defence Staff (IDS)
- **Personnel:** Drawn from Indian Army, Indian Navy, and Indian Air Force
- **Head:** Two-star rank officer (Air Vice Marshal / Rear Admiral / Major General)
- **Sub-organisations:**
  - **Defence Space Research Agency (DSRA)** — develops space-warfare systems and technologies
  - **Defence Imagery Processing and Analysis Centre** (subsumed, Delhi)
  - **Defence Satellite Control Centre** (subsumed, Bhopal)

## Key Programmes

### Anti-Satellite (ASAT) Programme
- **Origin:** Indian Ballistic Missile Defence (BMD) programme (started 1999)
- **First ASAT test:** 27 March 2019 (Mission Shakti)
- **Under development:** Directed energy, co-orbital, laser, and EMP-based ASAT weapons

### Space Based Surveillance (SBS) Project
- Constellation of satellites for earth observation, military surveillance, and communications
- **SBS Phase III:** 52 military communication and surveillance satellites planned for launch between 2025–2029
- **Three-layered constellation:** Low Earth Orbit, Medium Earth Orbit, and Geostationary Orbit

### Mission DefSpace (Oct 2022–)
- Launched under the iDEX framework to promote indigenous dual-use space technologies
- 75 "Defence Space Challenges" covering launch, satellite, communication, ground, and software systems
- Aligned with Atmanirbhar Bharat initiative
