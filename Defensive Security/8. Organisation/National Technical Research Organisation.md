# National Technical Research Organisation (NTRO)

> **Type:** Technical Intelligence Agency — Government of India
> **Established:** 2004
> **Headquarters:** New Delhi, India
> **Reports to:** National Security Advisor (NSA) → Prime Minister's Office (PMO)
> **Employees / Budget:** Classified
> **Website:** [ntro.gov.in](https://ntro.gov.in/)
> **Motto:** *āno bhadra krtavo yantu vishwatah* — Let noble thoughts come to me from all directions

---

## What It Does

NTRO is India's **premier technical intelligence gathering agency**, modelled on the US National Security Agency (NSA). It serves as a **super-feeder agency** providing technical intelligence (TECHINT) to other agencies on both internal and external security. Key capabilities include:

- **Satellite surveillance** — operating spy satellites (TES, Cartosat-2A/2B, EMISAT, RISAT-1, RISAT-2)
- **Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)** — intercepting and analysing communications
- **Internet monitoring** — tracking online threats to national security
- **Terrestrial monitoring** — ground-based surveillance operations
- **Geospatial intelligence (GEOINT)** — satellite imagery and mapping for military operations
- **Cryptology** — encryption/decryption research (via NICRD)
- **Remote sensing & data processing** — analysing intelligence from multiple sensor platforms
- **Strategic hardware & software development** — building custom surveillance tools
- **Ocean surveillance** — jointly operates INS Dhruv with the Indian Navy
- **Radar operations** — operates Very Long Range Tracking Radar (VLRTR) arrays at Udaipur, Bhopal, and Balasore with the IAF for missile monitoring and ballistic missile defence

NTRO has the same "norms of conduct" as the Intelligence Bureau (IB) and the Research and Analysis Wing (R&AW).

## History

| Date | Event |
|------|-------|
| **1999** | Kargil War — intelligence gathering weaknesses identified |
| **Oct 2001** | Roadmap for the National Technical Facilities Organisation (NTFO) prepared |
| **2001–02** | Group of Ministers recommended creation of NTFO |
| **Oct 2004** | Cabinet Committee on Security (CCS) approved setting up of NTRO |
| **Feb 2010** | First Indian intelligence agency to be audited by the Comptroller and Auditor General (CAG) |
| **May 2017** | NTRO brought under the Intelligence Organisations (Restriction of Rights) Act |

## Notable Operations

| Year | Operation | Details |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **2008–present** | **SIGINT Seniors Pacific (SSPAC)** | Active member of the US NSA-led 10-member counter-terrorism SIGINT platform for over a decade |
| **Dec 2014** | **Pakistani boat interception** | COMINT led Indian Coast Guard to intercept a Pakistani fishing boat in the Arabian Sea (~365 km from Porbandar); occupants suspected of planning a 26/11-type attack |
| **Sep 2016** | **2016 LoC Surgical Strike support** | Provided satellite intelligence using Cartosat-2C, Cartosat-1, and Resourcesat-2 to image terrorist training camps in Pakistan-administered Kashmir |
| **Feb 2019** | **Balakot Airstrike support** | Surveillance confirmed ~300 active targets via mobile connections at a terrorist training camp days before the IAF strike |
| **2025** | **Operation Sindoor** | Provided critical satellite imagery and electronic surveillance to identify, track, and verify terrorist hideout locations |

## Hierarchy & Career Structure

NTRO follows a **merit-based promotion system** (not seniority-based) for its Group A scientists. It is common for junior scientists to supersede seniors based on performance.

| Grade (Pay Level) | NTRO Posting | Govt. Equivalent |
|-------------------|-------------|------------------|
| Level 17 (Apex) | Chairman, NTRO | Secretary to GoI |
| Level 16 | Distinguished Scientist / Director General | Additional Secretary |
| Level 15 | Scientist 'H' / Director | Additional Secretary |
| Level 14 | Scientist 'G' / Associate Director | Joint Secretary |
| Level 13A | Scientist 'F' / Additional Director | Director |
| Level 13 | Scientist 'E' / Additional Director | Director |
| Level 12 | Scientist 'D' / Joint Director | Deputy Secretary |
| Level 11 | Scientist 'C' / Deputy Director | Under Secretary |
| Level 10 | Scientist 'B' / Assistant Director (Entry) | Assistant Secretary |

## Relationship with Other Agencies

- **Intelligence Bureau (IB)** and **R&AW** — same norms of conduct; historical turf wars with R&AW's Aviation Research Centre (ARC) over airborne intelligence assets
- **Indian Air Force** — co-operates VLRTR arrays for missile monitoring
- **Indian Navy** — jointly operates INS Dhruv (ocean surveillance ship)
- **ICERT / NIC** — past disputes over network access and penetration testing

## Related Organisations

- **Central Monitoring System (CMS)** — lawful interception and monitoring
- **Multi Agency Centre (MAC)** — intelligence coordination
