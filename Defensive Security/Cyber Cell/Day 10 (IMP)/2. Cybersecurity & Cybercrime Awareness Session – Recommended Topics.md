
# Cybersecurity & Cybercrime Awareness Session – Recommended Topics

## Objective

When organizing a **Cybersecurity and Cybercrime Awareness Session**, the content should be tailored to the target audience. Different age groups and user categories face different cyber risks.

---

# 1. Crime Against People (General Public)

Focus on cybercrimes that directly affect individuals.

Topics:

- Phishing attacks
    
- OTP fraud
    
- UPI fraud
    
- QR code scams
    
- Social media scams
    
- Identity theft
    
- SIM swap fraud
    
- Banking fraud
    
- Cyber stalking
    
- Online blackmail & sextortion
    
- Fake customer care scams
    
- Loan app fraud
    
- Job scams
    
- Fake shopping websites
    
- Online gaming fraud
    
- Cryptocurrency scams
    

---

# 2. General Citizens

Teach basic cyber hygiene.

Topics:

- Strong passwords
    
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
    
- Safe browsing
    
- Secure online payments
    
- Safe UPI practices
    
- Safe public Wi-Fi usage
    
- Device updates
    
- Mobile security
    
- Cloud backup
    
- Reporting cybercrime (1930 & National Cyber Crime Portal)
    
- Digital footprint awareness
    

---

# 3. Senior Citizens (Old People)

Senior citizens are frequently targeted due to lower digital literacy.

Topics:

- OTP scams
    
- KYC update fraud
    
- Fake bank calls
    
- Digital arrest scams
    
- WhatsApp impersonation
    
- Investment fraud
    
- Lottery scams
    
- Pension fraud
    
- Health insurance scams
    
- Remote access app scams
    
- AI voice scams
    
- Deepfake scams
    

Safety Tips:

- Never share OTP.
    
- Never install remote access apps on request.
    
- Verify bank calls independently.
    
- Contact family before making large payments.
    

---

# 4. Children (Below Class 8)

The focus should be on **digital safety rather than hacking**.

Topics:

- Safe internet habits
    
- Online strangers
    
- Gaming safety
    
- Cyberbullying
    
- Screen-time management
    
- Privacy settings
    
- Safe YouTube usage
    
- Fake giveaways
    
- In-app purchases
    
- Online predators
    
- Password safety
    

---

# 5. Age-Appropriate Apps & Games

Discuss:

- Age ratings (PEGI/ESRB)
    
- Safe gaming practices
    
- Voice chat risks
    
- Privacy settings
    
- Gaming addiction
    
- Parental controls
    

Examples:

- Roblox
    
- Minecraft
    
- BGMI
    
- Free Fire
    
- Fortnite
    

---

# 6. Digital Diet

Teach healthy technology usage.

Topics:

- Managing screen time
    
- Digital wellbeing
    
- Healthy sleep habits
    
- Social media balance
    
- Notification management
    
- Offline activities
    
- Family digital rules
    

---

# 7. Zero Social Media (Digital Minimalism)

Discuss:

- Reducing oversharing
    
- Limiting personal information online
    
- Benefits of fewer social media accounts
    
- Privacy-focused communication
    
- Digital footprint reduction
    

---

# 8. Digital Dementia

Explain how excessive digital device use may affect:

- Memory
    
- Attention span
    
- Concentration
    
- Learning habits
    

Discuss healthy technology habits and regular offline activities.

---

# 9. APK Safety

Explain:

- What APK files are
    
- Risks of installing APKs from unofficial websites
    
- Malware hidden inside APKs
    
- Permission abuse
    
- Fake banking apps
    
- Fake government apps
    

Best Practice:

Install apps only from:

- Google Play Store
    
- Apple App Store
    
- Official developer websites (when appropriate)
    

---

# 10. Government Service Frauds

Examples:

- Fake gas connection updates
    
- Fake electricity bill messages
    
- Fake traffic challan messages
    
- Fake pension verification
    
- Fake Aadhaar update links
    
- Fake PAN update requests
    
- Fake income tax refund messages
    

Teach participants to verify requests through official government websites or apps.

---

# 11. Financial Scams

Cover:

- Banking fraud
    
- Credit card fraud
    
- QR code scams
    
- UPI scams
    
- KYC scams
    
- Loan scams
    
- Fake payment links
    
- Refund scams
    

---

# 12. Investment Scams

Common frauds include:

- Guaranteed returns
    
- Double-your-money schemes
    
- Crypto investment fraud
    
- Ponzi schemes
    
- Fake IPOs
    
- Fake mutual funds
    
- AI trading scams
    

Warning signs:

- "Guaranteed profits"
    
- "No risk"
    
- Pressure to invest immediately
    

---

# 13. Share Market & Trading Frauds

Topics:

- Fake stock advisory groups
    
- Telegram trading scams
    
- Insider tips fraud
    
- Pump-and-dump schemes
    
- Fake SEBI registration
    
- WhatsApp investment groups
    

---

# 14. "Invest ₹1,000 and Earn ₹10,000" Scams

Explain how fraudsters:

- Show fake profits initially.
    
- Encourage larger investments.
    
- Block withdrawals.
    
- Disappear after collecting money.
    

Teach participants to verify platforms before investing.

---

# 15. Digital Arrest Scam

Explain the scam:

Fraudsters impersonate:

- Police
    
- CBI
    
- ED
    
- RBI
    
- Customs
    
- Income Tax officials
    

Victims are falsely told they are under investigation and pressured into transferring money.

Key message:

**No genuine law enforcement agency conducts arrests or investigations over video calls while demanding immediate payment.**

---

# 16. Safe Online Booking

Always use:

- Official company websites
    
- Official mobile applications
    
- Trusted travel platforms
    

Avoid:

- Links received on WhatsApp
    
- Unknown advertisements
    
- Fake customer care numbers
    
- Sponsored search results without verification
    

Examples of trusted platforms include well-known travel companies such as MakeMyTrip, IRCTC, airline websites, and hotel chains—always verify that you are on the official website before making a payment.

---

# 17. Reporting Cybercrime

Teach participants how to report incidents:

- National Cyber Crime Helpline: **1930**
    
- National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal
    
- Local Police / Cyber Police Station
    
- Bank's fraud helpline
    

Report immediately to improve the chances of preventing financial loss.

---

# Key Takeaways

- Awareness programs should be tailored to the audience (children, adults, senior citizens, professionals).
    
- Focus on practical cyber hygiene and the most common scams affecting each group.
    
- Teach participants to verify information through **official websites and trusted applications** before making payments or sharing personal information.
    
- Promote immediate reporting of cyber fraud and encourage a culture of verification rather than trust.
