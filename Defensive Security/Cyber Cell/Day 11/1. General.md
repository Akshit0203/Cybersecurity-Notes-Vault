## Indian Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (NCRP)

- Official Government of India portal to report cybercrime.
- Managed by the **Ministry of Home Affairs (MHA)** under the **Indian Cyber Crime Coordination Centre (I4C)**.
- Used to report:
    - Online financial fraud
    - Social media crimes
    - Cyber stalking
    - Identity theft
    - Child exploitation
    - Phishing and other cyber offences
- For financial fraud, immediately call **1930** and report on the portal to improve the chances of freezing fraudulent transactions

## Sanchar Saathi

- Citizen-centric portal launched by the **Department of Telecommunications (DoT)**.
- Helps users:
    - Block and trace lost/stolen mobile phones (CEIR)
    - Check mobile connections issued in their name (TAFCOP)
    - Report suspicious or fraudulent telecom activity
- Aims to reduce telecom fraud, SIM misuse, identity theft, and cyber-enabled financial crime.
