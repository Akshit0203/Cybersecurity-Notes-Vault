
# Gurugram Police, Cambodia & Cyber Slavery

# 1. What is Cyber Slavery?

**Cyber slavery** is a form of **human trafficking** in which victims are lured abroad with fake job offers and then forced to commit cyber fraud under threats, violence, or confinement.

Victims are commonly promised jobs such as:

- Data Entry Operator
    
- Customer Support Executive
    
- Digital Marketing Executive
    
- IT Support
    
- Call Centre Executive
    
- Sales Executive
    

After arriving, many victims have their passports confiscated and are coerced into conducting cyber scams.

---

# 2. Countries Frequently Reported in Cyber Slavery Cases

Several investigations have linked cyber slavery operations to scam compounds in:

- Cambodia
    
- Myanmar
    
- Laos
    
- Thailand (often used as a transit route)
    

These operations are typically run by **transnational organized crime groups**.

---

# 3. Gurugram Police Investigations

The **Gurugram Police Cyber Cell** has investigated multiple cross-border cyber fraud and human trafficking cases.

Investigations have uncovered:

- Recruitment of Indian youth using fake overseas job offers.
    
- Victims trafficked to **Cambodia** and **Myanmar**.
    
- Forced participation in cyber fraud operations.
    
- Organized networks involving recruiters, facilitators, and financial handlers.

# 5. Common Frauds Performed

Victims have reportedly been forced to carry out:

- Digital Arrest scams
    
- Investment scams
    
- Cryptocurrency scams
    
- Pig Butchering scams
    
- Romance scams
    
- WhatsApp fraud
    
- Fake customer support scams
    
- Phishing campaigns
    

---

# 6. Role of Organized Crime

Investigations indicate that many scam compounds are operated by **transnational organized crime groups**, with recruiters and facilitators working across multiple countries.

These networks often involve:

- Local recruiters
    
- Travel facilitators
    
- Money mules
    
- Cryptocurrency handlers
    
- International coordinators
    

---

# 7. Gurugram Police Case Example

In one investigation, Gurugram Police:

- Arrested individuals connected to cyber fraud networks.
    
- Seized illegal **SIM boxes**, routers, and hundreds of SIM cards.
    
- Identified links to **Cambodia** and the **Philippines**.
    
- Reported evidence that some accused were recruiting Indians for **cyber slavery** operations.
    

In another case, Gurugram Police arrested recruiters accused of trafficking Indian youth to **Myanmar**, where victims were allegedly forced into cyber fraud after being promised jobs in Thailand.

---

# 8. Red Flags for Overseas Job Offers

Be cautious if a recruiter:

- Promises unusually high salaries.
    
- Requires payment for recruitment.
    
- Uses only Telegram or WhatsApp for communication.
    
- Provides vague job descriptions.
    
- Arranges tourist visas instead of work visas.
    
- Refuses to share employer details.
    
- Pressures you to travel immediately.
    

---

# 9. Prevention

Before accepting an overseas job:

- Verify the employer's identity.
    
- Use licensed recruitment agencies.
    
- Confirm visa and employment documents.
    
- Inform family of your travel plans.
    
- Verify the job through official government channels where applicable.
    

---

# 10. Key Takeaways

- **Cyber slavery** combines **human trafficking** with **forced participation in cybercrime**.
    
- Victims are often lured with fake overseas job offers and transported to scam compounds in countries such as **Cambodia** and **Myanmar**.
    
- **Gurugram Police** has investigated multiple cases involving cyber fraud networks with links to Southeast Asia, including arrests related to recruitment, SIM-box operations, and alleged cyber slavery.
    
- Cyber slavery is a growing transnational crime that requires cooperation between law enforcement, immigration authorities, financial investigators, and international agencies.
