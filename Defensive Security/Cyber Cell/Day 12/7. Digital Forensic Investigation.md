3. Triage Step A: Initial Triage (Powered ON vs. Powered OFF)

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM%20(6).jpeg)

**Content / Notes:**

- **DEVICE IS ON:**
    - **DO NOT turn off**.
    - Check for destructive processes (pull the power cable if wiping is detected).
    - Isolate from networks and disable screensavers/auto-lock.
    - Check for active encryption (e.g., BitLocker, FileVault, VeraCrypt).
- **DEVICE IS OFF:**
    - **DO NOT turn on**, as there is a risk of altering data or triggering deletion.
    - Ask the suspect for the PIN/Password.
    - Seize, document, and secure the device. **Where to paste this:** Paste this as **Phase 3**, under **"First Contact & Initial Triage."**

4. Triage Step B: Device Handling Flowchart

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM%20(1).jpeg)

**Content / Notes:**

- **If the Device is ON:**
    - **Can you access the system? (YES):** Engage Airplane Mode, extend the screen timeout, **DO NOT turn it off**, keep it charged, and transport it to the lab.
    - **Can you access the system? (NO):** **DO NOT turn it off**, keep it charged, and transport it to the lab.
- **If the Device is OFF:**
    - **DO NOT turn it on**. Collect identifiers, place the exhibit in a Faraday Bag (if available), and transport it to the lab.
- **Operational Note:** The Android ecosystem is heavily fragmented, unlike iOS. Investigators must be prepared for proprietary data connections and drive inconsistencies across different manufacturers. **Where to paste this:** Paste this immediately after the Initial Triage section, perhaps as a sub-section titled **"Step-by-Step Handling Flowchart."**

6. Volatile Data Collection: Live Analysis Target Matrix

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM%20(4).jpeg)

_(Note: This step only occurs if the device was found powered ON and accessible, prior to pulling the plug)._ **Content / Notes:**

- **Order of Volatility:**
    - **High Volatility:** RAM content, Routing tables, ARP cache, DNS cache (Tools: DumpIt, fmem, route PRINT).
    - **Medium Volatility:** Open files, running processes, active network connections (Tools: netstat, PsList, lsof).
    - **Low Volatility:** Disk content, system configuration.
- **Methodology Note:** Always use tools with the smallest system footprint. Never save captured data to the suspect's media; always use external, sterile storage. Utilize tools to detect encryption or cloud storage before pulling the plug. **Where to paste this:** Paste this as **Phase 5**, under **"Live System Analysis & Volatile Data Collection."**

7. Imaging: Acquisition (The Forensic Copy)

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM%20(3).jpeg)

**Content / Notes:**

- **Golden Rule:** NEVER ANALYZE THE ORIGINAL DEVICE.
- **Data Integrity Pipeline:** Data must flow from the Source Device through a **Hardware Write Blocker** to the Destination Device.
- **Method 1: Clone (Device to Device):** An exact, physical bit-by-bit replica onto a previously wiped device of equal or greater capacity.
- **Method 2: Image (Device to File):** Compiles the exact replica into files (e.g., dd or E01 formats), allowing for compression, encryption, and metadata inclusion.
- **Method 3: Logical Copy (File to File):** Used when physical copying is impossible, such as extracting an unencrypted volume from a live, encrypted machine. **Where to paste this:** Paste this as **Phase 6**, under **"Data Acquisition & Forensic Imaging."**

8. Integrity: Cryptographic Verification (Hashing)

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM%20(2).jpeg)

**Content / Notes:**

- **Key Concepts:**
    - _The Digital Fingerprint:_ A mathematical algorithm calculates a unique signature for a dataset. If a single bit changes, the hash changes entirely.
    - _Cross-referencing:_ Hashes allow investigators to detect known files (like CSAM or malware) via NSRL databases without viewing the files.
- The Origin Hash and Destination Hash must be a **PERFECT MATCH** after acquisition.
- **Modern Hardware Warning:** Solid-State Drives (SSDs) possess self-purging mechanisms. If left powered on without an interface, an SSD can unilaterally delete data, permanently altering its hash. **Where to paste this:** Paste this as **Phase 7**, under **"Data Integrity & Hash Verification."**

9. Conclusion: Synthesis & The Admissibility Architecture

![](../Day%2011/attachments/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-06-20%20at%209.09.05%20PM.jpeg)

**Content / Notes:**

- Successful digital forensics relies on an **unbroken chain of custody and verifiable integrity**. Never let the pursuit of data compromise the evidence itself.
- The foundation of judicial admissibility is built strictly in this order (from bottom to top):
    1. Meticulous Planning & Equipment Prep
    2. On-Scene Triage & Network Isolation
    3. Write-Blocked Forensic Copying
    4. Cryptographic Hashing (Verification)
    5. **Judicial Admissibility** (The ultimate goal) **Where to paste this:** Paste this at the very end of your document as the **"Conclusion"** or **"Summary of Legal Admissibility."**
