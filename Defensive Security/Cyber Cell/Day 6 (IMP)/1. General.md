**BHARATPOL** is an Indian crime-monitoring and investigation portal launched by the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) to connect state and central law enforcement agencies directly with INTERPOL. It allows Indian police forces to coordinate with international authorities in real-time, streamline Red Notices, and track transnational criminals. 

The portal decentralizes access to INTERPOL databases, which was previously restricted only to the CBI. It is primarily designed to combat transnational crimes, including cybercrime, economic fraud, drug trafficking, and human trafficking. 
Key Modules

The BHARATPOL platform features five main modules: 
- **Connect:** Links the CBI (as the National Central Bureau) with all state and local police authorities on a single domestic platform.
- **INTERPOL Notices:** Streamlines the processing and tracking of Red Corner Notices and other color-coded INTERPOL alerts.
- **References:** Acts as a database repository for international criminal inquiries and historical case files.
- **Broadcast:** Enables rapid, structured transmission of requests for assistance between Indian and foreign law enforcement agencies.
- **Resources:** Provides access to a centralized directory of global policing guidelines, laws, and investigative tools.

To explore official resources or learn more about this platform, visit the [BHARATPOL Portal](https://bharatpol.cbi.gov.in/) directly.
