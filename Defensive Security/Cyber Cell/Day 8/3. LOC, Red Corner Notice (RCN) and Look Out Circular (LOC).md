
# LOC, Red Corner Notice (RCN) and Look Out Circular (LOC)
# 1. LOC (Look Out Circular)

## Definition

A **Look Out Circular (LOC)** is an alert issued by Indian authorities to immigration agencies and border control officials to monitor or intercept a person entering or leaving the country.

It is commonly used when a person is:

- Wanted for investigation
    
- Accused in a criminal case
    
- Evading law enforcement
    
- Required to appear before a court or investigating agency
    

---

## Purpose

The objective of an LOC is to:

- Prevent a suspect from leaving India
    
- Detect the arrival of a wanted person
    
- Inform the issuing agency when the person is located at an immigration checkpoint
    

---

## Example Workflow

```text
Police / Investigating Agency
            │
            ▼
Request for LOC
            │
            ▼
Immigration Authorities
            │
            ▼
Person Arrives at Airport
            │
            ▼
LOC Match Found
            │
            ▼
Agency Informed
            │
            ▼
Action Taken According to Law
```

---

# 2. Red Corner Notice (RCN)

## Definition

A **Red Corner Notice (RCN)** is a notice published by **INTERPOL** at the request of a member country to help locate and provisionally detain a wanted person for possible extradition or similar legal action.

---

## Purpose

An RCN helps member countries:

- Locate fugitives
    
- Share identifying information
    
- Facilitate international cooperation
    
- Support extradition proceedings where applicable
    

---

## Important Clarification

A **Red Corner Notice is not an international arrest warrant.**

Each country decides independently whether and how to act on an RCN under its own laws.

---

## Example Workflow

```text
Country Requests RCN
          │
          ▼
INTERPOL Reviews Request
          │
          ▼
Red Corner Notice Published
          │
          ▼
Member Countries Receive Notice
          │
          ▼
Wanted Person Located
          │
          ▼
Local Authorities Act Under Domestic Law
```

---

# 3. LOC vs Red Corner Notice

|Feature|Look Out Circular (LOC)|Red Corner Notice (RCN)|
|---|---|---|
|Scope|Primarily within India|International|
|Issued By|Indian authorities|INTERPOL at the request of a member country|
|Main Purpose|Monitor/intercept travel through Indian immigration|Help locate and provisionally detain fugitives internationally|
|Arrest Guarantee|No|No – action depends on local law|
|Used At|Airports, seaports, land borders in India|INTERPOL member countries|

---

# 4. Other INTERPOL Notices

|Notice|Purpose|
|---|---|
|**Red Notice**|Locate and provisionally detain wanted persons|
|**Blue Notice**|Collect information about a person's identity, location, or activities|
|**Yellow Notice**|Help locate missing persons or identify persons unable to identify themselves|
|**Black Notice**|Identify unknown deceased persons|
|**Green Notice**|Warn about individuals who may pose a threat or have committed crimes in multiple countries|
|**Orange Notice**|Warn about dangerous events, objects, or concealed threats|
|**Purple Notice**|Share information on criminal methods, techniques, or concealment|
|**Silver Notice** _(where implemented)_|Assist in tracing and identifying criminally derived assets|

---

# 5. Key Takeaways

- A **Look Out Circular (LOC)** is an Indian immigration alert used to monitor or intercept individuals at border checkpoints.
    
- A **Red Corner Notice (RCN)** is an INTERPOL notice requesting member countries to help locate and provisionally detain a wanted person for possible extradition.
    
- A **Red Corner Notice is not itself an arrest warrant**; enforcement depends on the laws and decisions of the country where the person is found.
    
- INTERPOL also issues other color-coded notices (Blue, Yellow, Green, Orange, Purple, etc.) for different investigative and public safety purposes.
