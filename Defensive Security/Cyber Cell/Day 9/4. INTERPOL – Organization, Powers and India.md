
# INTERPOL – Organization, Powers and India

# 1. Do INTERPOL Officers Meet Psychologists?

INTERPOL has employee wellness and mental health support programs, especially for personnel exposed to disturbing material (such as child exploitation, terrorism, or violent crime investigations).

However:

- There is **no publicly documented rule** stating that **every INTERPOL officer must meet a psychologist every two weeks**.
    
- Mental health support is available, but the exact frequency depends on the role, internal policies, and operational needs.
    

---

# 2. Who Funds INTERPOL?

INTERPOL is funded primarily through:

- Mandatory annual contributions from member countries.
    
- Voluntary contributions from governments.
    
- Grants from international organizations and approved partners.
    

Currently, INTERPOL has **196 member countries** that contribute to its budget according to an agreed funding model.

---

# 3. Does INTERPOL Have Police Powers?

**No.**

INTERPOL is **not an international police force.**

INTERPOL officers:

- Cannot arrest suspects.
    
- Cannot conduct raids.
    
- Cannot execute search warrants.
    
- Cannot seize evidence.
    
- Cannot independently investigate crimes inside a country.
    

---

# 4. Role of INTERPOL

INTERPOL acts as a coordination and information-sharing organization.

Its functions include:

- International police cooperation.
    
- Sharing intelligence.
    
- Issuing notices (e.g., Red Notice).
    
- Criminal database access.
    
- Coordinating cross-border investigations.
    
- Supporting extradition requests.
    

---

# 5. How INTERPOL Operates

```text
Country A Police
        │
        ▼
National Central Bureau (NCB)
        │
        ▼
INTERPOL General Secretariat
        │
        ▼
NCB of Country B
        │
        ▼
Country B Police
```

INTERPOL facilitates communication—it does not replace national police agencies.

---

# 6. National Central Bureau (NCB)

Every INTERPOL member country has an **NCB (National Central Bureau)**.

In India:

- The **Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI)** serves as India's National Central Bureau (NCB) for INTERPOL.
    

---

# 7. Can INTERPOL Conduct Operations in Another Country?

**No.**

INTERPOL cannot:

- Enter another country independently.
    
- Arrest suspects.
    
- Carry out police operations.
    
- Exercise law enforcement powers.
    

All operational actions are performed by the country's own police or authorized agencies.

---

# 8. October 2022 – What Happened in India?

From **18–21 October 2022**, **India hosted the 90th INTERPOL General Assembly in New Delhi**.

This was a significant international law enforcement event attended by delegations from around the world.

---

# 9. Highlights of the 90th General Assembly

The event included:

- Delegations from **195 INTERPOL member countries** (at the time).
    
- Police chiefs.
    
- Ministers.
    
- Heads of National Central Bureaus (NCBs).
    
- Senior law enforcement officials.
    

Major discussion topics included:

- Cybercrime
    
- Terrorism
    
- Financial crime
    
- Organized crime
    
- Human trafficking
    
- Cryptocurrency investigations
    
- Child sexual exploitation
    
- Digital policing
    
- International cooperation
    

---

# 10. INTERPOL Metaverse

During the 2022 General Assembly, INTERPOL unveiled its **first law enforcement-focused Metaverse**, intended as a platform for training, collaboration, and exploring future policing technologies.

---

# 11. Why Was the 2022 General Assembly Important for India?

The event:

- Strengthened India's international law enforcement cooperation.
    
- Showcased India's role in combating transnational crime.
    
- Enabled collaboration on cybercrime, terrorism, financial fraud, and digital investigations.
    
- Brought together global police leaders for policy discussions.
    

---

# 12. Key Takeaways

- INTERPOL is an **international police cooperation organization**, **not an international police force**.
    
- It is funded mainly through contributions from its **196 member countries**.
    
- INTERPOL cannot independently arrest people or conduct operations inside another country.
    
- Every member country has a **National Central Bureau (NCB)**; in India, this role is performed by the **CBI**.
    
- India hosted the **90th INTERPOL General Assembly** in **New Delhi from 18–21 October 2022**, where global leaders discussed cybercrime, terrorism, financial crime, digital policing, and other international security challenges.
