
# CISO Incident Response, CERT-In 6-Hour Reporting, Advisory, SBOM & CBOM

# 1. Scenario

**Situation:**

You are the **Chief Information Security Officer (CISO)** of an organization.

A major cybersecurity incident has occurred, and the media informs you that they will publish the story in **10 minutes**.

What should you do?

---

# 2. Immediate Priorities

The first priority is **not** responding to the media.

The priority is to:

- Contain the incident.
    
- Activate the Incident Response Team (IRT).
    
- Notify management.
    
- Preserve evidence.
    
- Assess the impact.
    
- Begin legal and regulatory notifications.
    

---

# 4. CERT-In Reporting Requirement

Under the **CERT-In Directions, 2022**, certain cyber incidents must be reported to **CERT-In within 6 hours** of becoming aware of the incident.

Examples of reportable incidents include:

- Data breaches
    
- Ransomware attacks
    
- Website defacement
    
- Malware outbreaks
    
- DDoS attacks
    
- Unauthorized access
    
- Critical system compromise
    

The objective is to enable national coordination and help prevent similar attacks on other organizations.

---

# 5. Why Notify CERT-In?

CERT-In can:

- Issue national advisories.
    
- Share Indicators of Compromise (IOCs).
    
- Alert other organizations.
    
- Coordinate technical response.
    
- Reduce the spread of attacks.
    
- Improve national cyber resilience.
    

Example:

```text
Company A Breached
        │
        ▼
CERT-In Receives Report
        │
        ▼
Threat Analysis
        │
        ▼
National Advisory Issued
        │
        ▼
Other Organizations Patch Systems
```

---

# 6. Handling the Media

If contacted by the media:

Do **not** speculate or disclose unverified information.

A typical response should be:

- Acknowledge awareness of the incident.
    
- Confirm that the investigation is ongoing.
    
- State that affected stakeholders and regulators are being informed where required.
    
- Commit to providing updates once verified information is available.
    

---

# 7. CERT-In Advisory

After analyzing significant incidents, CERT-In may publish advisories that include:

- Description of the threat
    
- Affected products
    
- Indicators of Compromise (IOCs)
    
- Mitigation steps
    
- Security recommendations
    
- Patch information
    

Organizations use these advisories to strengthen their defenses.

# 8. SBOM (Software Bill of Materials)

## Definition

An **SBOM** is a machine-readable inventory of all software components that make up an application.

It typically lists:

- Libraries
    
- Frameworks
    
- Open-source dependencies
    
- Versions
    
- Suppliers
    
- Licenses
    

Example:

```text
Application

├── OpenSSL 3.2
├── Log4j 2.20
├── Spring Boot
├── React
└── SQLite
```

---

# 9. Why SBOM is Important

SBOM helps organizations:

- Identify vulnerable components quickly.
    
- Respond to supply chain attacks.
    
- Track software dependencies.
    
- Improve vulnerability management.
    
- Meet compliance requirements.
    

Example:

```text
New CVE Published

↓

Search SBOM

↓

Affected Component Found

↓

Patch Immediately
```

---

# 10. CBOM (Cryptography Bill of Materials)

## Definition

A **CBOM** is an inventory of the cryptographic assets used within software or systems.

It may include:

- Encryption algorithms
    
- Key sizes
    
- Certificates
    
- Cryptographic libraries
    
- Key management mechanisms
    

Example:

```text
Application

├── AES-256
├── RSA-3072
├── SHA-256
├── TLS 1.3
└── OpenSSL
```

---

# 11. Why CBOM is Important

CBOM enables organizations to:

- Identify weak or deprecated cryptography.
    
- Prepare for cryptographic migrations.
    
- Support post-quantum cryptography planning.
    
- Improve cryptographic governance.
    

---

# 12. CISO Responsibilities During an Incident

The CISO should:

- Lead the incident response.
    
- Coordinate technical teams.
    
- Notify executives.
    
- Ensure regulatory reporting.
    
- Preserve forensic evidence.
    
- Coordinate with CERT-In (where applicable).
    
- Manage communication with legal, public relations, and stakeholders.
    
- Oversee recovery and post-incident improvements.
    

---

# 13. Key Takeaways

- A CISO's first responsibility during a cyber incident is to **contain the attack and coordinate incident response**, not to respond immediately to media inquiries.
    
- **CERT-In** requires certain cyber incidents to be reported **within 6 hours** of becoming aware of them under the 2022 Directions.
    
- CERT-In uses reported incidents to issue **national advisories**, helping other organizations defend against similar threats.
    
- An **SBOM (Software Bill of Materials)** provides a complete inventory of software components and dependencies.
    
- A **CBOM (Cryptography Bill of Materials)** inventories the cryptographic algorithms, libraries, certificates, and related assets used within a system, supporting cryptographic risk management and future migration planning.
