# **Actionable takeaways from 1-billion-views UGC playbook**

The biggest lesson is that **growth should be treated as an experimentation system, not a creative gamble.**

Approach is built around producing many variations, tracking results, identifying repeatable winners, and scaling what works.

Here's what I'd actually implement, especially if you're planning to build a cybersecurity company or launch a tech product.

## **1. The 10 actionable things worth implementing**

|#|Action|What to actually do|
|---|---|---|
|1|Find a visual hook|Identify a product feature that can be demonstrated in 3–5 seconds.|
|2|Simplify the message|Explain what the product does and how it changes someone's life in one sentence.|
|3|Recruit micro-creators|Start with multiple affordable creators instead of one expensive influencer.|
|4|Test different audiences|Test different creator types, demographics, regions and languages.|
|5|Build a creative matrix|Test creator × format × angle × visual hook.|
|6|Prioritise volume|Start with 30–50 videos monthly, then increase when your workflow can handle it.|
|7|Track video-level metrics|Record views, retention, clicks, installs, conversions and cost.|
|8|Give fast feedback|Review new content daily and give creators specific improvement instructions.|
|9|Reuse winning content|Turn proven organic videos into paid campaigns, with appropriate usage rights.|
|10|Retain winning creators|Build relationships, pay promptly and offer bonuses for good performance.|

## **2. Build a repeatable content experimentation system**

This is probably the most valuable framework in the entire post.

**The UGC experimentation formula**

**Creator**

Who delivers the content?

Students, professionals, tech creators

**Format**

How is it presented?

Tutorial, reaction, story, demonstration

**Angle**

What is the message?

Problem, solution, benefit, curiosity

**Visual hook**

What stops scrolling?

Screen recording, reveal, demonstration

**Publish → Measure → Compare → Improve**

Scale repeatable winners

Don't just publish 20 similar videos. Publish variations that test different hypotheses.

## **3. Set up a tracking spreadsheet**

The post specifically recommends recording these fields before publishing:

- Creator and creator type
    
- Audience
    
- Angle
    
- Format
    
- Hook
    
- Post date
    
- 48-hour views
    
- Retention
    
- Clicks, installs and purchases
    
- Revenue and cost
    
- Usage rights and paid promotion status
    

Useful formulas:

\[ CPM = \frac{\text{Total Cost}}{\text{Views}}\times1000 \]

\[ Conversion\ Rate = \frac{\text{Conversions}}{\text{Clicks}}\times100 \]

Don't judge a creator solely on views. A video with fewer views but better conversions may be more valuable.

## **4. Weekly operating system**

Suggests a weekly production and optimisation cycle:

|Day|Action|
|---|---|
|Monday|Analyse video-level performance|
|Tuesday|Cut weak formats and scale winning ones|
|Wednesday|Brief new variations of successful ideas|
|Thursday|Produce and test variations|
|Friday|Update content library with winning hooks, formats and creators|
|Daily|Review new videos and give feedback|

### **B. Your future cybersecurity company**

This is where the actual UGC strategy becomes interesting.

Suppose you launch an AI security SaaS product.

You could:

1. Recruit 10–20 micro-creators in cybersecurity and technology.
    
2. Give them access to your product.
    
3. Ask them to demonstrate different use cases.
    
4. Test educational, problem-solution, tutorial and reaction formats.
    
5. Identify which audience and messaging generates actual signups.
    
6. Scale the best-performing creator-format combinations.
    

A particularly interesting opportunity is targeting international audiences through Indian technology creators, as the original post suggests.

## **6. What I would NOT blindly follow**

A few things need perspective:

- **1,000 videos/month:** Excessive for an individual creator or an early-stage company without production capacity.
    
- **$2 CPM:** Treat this as the author's reported result, not a guaranteed benchmark.
    
- **Views as the primary success metric:** For a cybersecurity SaaS, qualified leads, trials and paying customers matter more.
    
- **TikTok-specific tactics:** Don't assume Spark Ads, creator tagging and TikTok distribution translate directly to LinkedIn.
    
- **Hiring creators immediately:** First validate that the product and its messaging are worth promoting.
