### the reading list: this is the one that actually matters

if i could force-feed you one category it's this. reading how _real_ bugs get found rewires your brain in a way no lab can.

|thing|my take|
|---|---|
|★ [HackerOne Hacktivity](https://hackerone.com/hacktivity)|public disclosed bug bounty reports. read a few hundred and you slowly grow a _nose_: you start going "i bet there's an IDOR here" before you even look.|
|★ [@disclosedh1](https://x.com/disclosedh1) (on X)|a bot that posts newly-disclosed HackerOne reports as they drop. just follow it and let real bugs drift through your timeline forever. laziest, highest-roi follow there is.|
|★ [Google Project Zero](https://googleprojectzero.blogspot.com/)|the best offensive research team on earth writing up their work. hard, humbling, brilliant. you won't get all of it at first. read it anyway.|
|[PortSwigger Research](https://portswigger.net/research)|James Kettle & co. literally inventing new classes of web attack. watch techniques appear that didn't exist yesterday.|
|[Pwnie Awards](https://pwnies.com/)|basically a curated reading list of the year's most interesting (and cursed) bugs.|
|incident reports & postmortems|when a big company gets breached and writes it up. how one tiny misconfig becomes a catastrophe. war stories, no war.|

### staying current: news, feeds & people worth following

security moves fast, and twitter rots your brain (more on that later), so curate _hard_. a few channels and feeds that are worth the noise:

|thing|my take|
|---|---|
|★ [Mental Outlaw](https://www.youtube.com/@MentalOutlaw)|privacy, opsec and security news with a healthy dose of "you're probably doing it wrong". chronically based and genuinely informative.|
|[Seytonic](https://www.youtube.com/@Seytonic)|weekly hacking news, breaches and cursed hardware. the comfiest way to keep up without doomscrolling.|
|[LowLevel](https://www.youtube.com/@LowLevelTV)|breaks down real CVEs and how exploits actually work, at a level that respects your brain.|
|[The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/) / [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/)|the daily "what got popped today" sites. skim headlines, click when curious.|
|[Hacker News](https://news.ycombinator.com/) (the orange one)|not security-specific, but the comments on any breach/vuln post are often better than the article itself.|
|★ [r/netsec](https://www.reddit.com/r/netsec/) & [r/cybersecurity](https://www.reddit.com/r/cybersecurity/)|firehoses of research, writeups and tools. two of the few subreddits still actually worth browsing.|
|good people on X|follow researchers who post writeups, mute everyone doing drama. [@LiveOverflow](https://x.com/LiveOverflow), [@NahamSec](https://x.com/NahamSec), [@Jhaddix](https://x.com/Jhaddix), [@stokfredrik](https://x.com/stokfredrik) are good threads to start pulling.|
