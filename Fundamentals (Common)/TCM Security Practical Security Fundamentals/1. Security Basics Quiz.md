# Security Basics — Check-In Quiz

> [!question] Q1: A hacker breached a system and was able to modify the logs to delete any traces of the actions they took, this constitutes a ______?
> - [ ] Failure of the firewall
> - [ ] Failure of Integrity
> - [ ] Failure of Confidentiality
> - [ ] Failure of Security Controls
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Failure of Integrity**
> </details>

> [!question] Q2: A company's database containing customer emails and passwords was leaked to the public because it was not encrypted at rest, which best describes this situation?
> - [ ] Failure of Confidentiality
> - [ ] Failure of Integrity
> - [ ] A failure of Availability
> - [ ] All of the above
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Failure of Confidentiality**
> </details>

> [!question] Q3: Which type of cyber attack generates the most revenue for cyber criminals?
> - [ ] Phishing
> - [ ] Breaches
> - [ ] Extortion
> - [ ] Ransomware
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Ransomware**
> </details>

> [!question] Q4: Your leadership is reviewing budgets and considering cutting Cloudflare, a cloud based protection against DDoS attacks. Which point of the CIA triad would this impact?
> - [ ] Confidentiality
> - [ ] Integrity
> - [ ] Availability
> - [ ] All of the above
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Availability**
> </details>

> [!question] Q5: Which statement completes this sentence "Cybersecurity ___________________"
> - [ ] Enables trust
> - [ ] Is integral to a modern and digital world
> - [ ] Can be required by law and regulation
> - [ ] All of the statements complete the sentence
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **All of the statements complete the sentence**
> </details>
