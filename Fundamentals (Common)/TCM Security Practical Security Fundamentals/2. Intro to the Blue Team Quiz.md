## Check-In Quiz

> [!question] Q1: Your organization has suffered a major breach and ransomware is spreading, which of the following blue team roles would most likely be responsible for responding to and containing the breach?
> - [ ] Level 1 SOC Analyst
> - [ ] Level 2 SOC Analyst
> - [ ] Incident Responder
> - [ ] Ransomware Negotiator
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Incident Responder**
> </details>

> [!question] Q2: Nowadays when someone refers to the SOC, they are most likely referring to the physical room in an office that houses the security team and their tools (true or false)
> - [ ] True
> - [ ] False
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **False**
> </details>

> [!question] Q3: Akshan works for a large organization, his day to day responsibilities include installing and configuring security hardware and software like firewalls and intrusion detection systems. Which job title best describes his responsibilities?
> - [ ] SOC Analyst
> - [ ] Incident Responder
> - [ ] Firewall Engineer
> - [ ] Security Engineer
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Security Engineer**
> </details>

> [!question] Q4: Which of the following statements about SOC analysts responsibilities are true?
> - [ ] SOC analysts usually document and track their work through a ticketing system
> - [ ] SOC analysts review and triage alerts
> - [ ] SOC analysts write documentation
> - [ ] All of the above
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **All of the above**
> </details>

> [!question] Q5: A Level 1 SOC analyst has completed their initial triage and based on their initial analysis and the playbook they are following, the next steps are too complex for their role. What are they likely to do in this scenario?
> - [ ] Perform research and educate themselves on how to perform the next tasks
> - [ ] Close the ticket and alert because it's too complex
> - [ ] Escalate the alert and ticket to a higher level SOC analyst
> - [ ] Call in an incident responder to contain the situation
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Escalate the alert and ticket to a higher level SOC analyst**
> </details>
