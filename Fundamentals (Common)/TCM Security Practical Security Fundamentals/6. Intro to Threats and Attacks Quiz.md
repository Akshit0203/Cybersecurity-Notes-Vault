# Intro to Threats and Attacks — Check-In Quiz

> [!question] Q1: A threat hunter in your organization was scanning the dark web and came across what appeared to be an employee offering to sell their VPN credentials, what best describes this?
> - [ ] An insider threat
> - [ ] A bad actor
> - [ ] A threat actor
> - [ ] An APT
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **An insider threat**
> </details>

> [!question] Q2: A threat actor began working on a popular open source project and after years of adding features secretly added in a backdoor, this is an example of?
> - [ ] An open-source attack
> - [ ] A supply-chain attack
> - [ ] An APT
> - [ ] A web attack
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **A supply-chain attack**
> </details>

> [!question] Q3: An attacker entered a building and left behind a malicious wireless access point that had an identical SSID to the company's wireless network, which best describes this?
> - [ ] A rogue access point
> - [ ] A spoofing attack
> - [ ] A MITM attack
> - [ ] An Evil-Twin access point
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **An Evil-Twin access point**
> </details>

> [!question] Q4: An attacker captures network traffic and then reviews it to look for confidential details such as passwords, this is an example of?
> - [ ] Sniffing
> - [ ] Spoofing
> - [ ] MITM
> - [ ] Jacking
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Sniffing**
> </details>

> [!question] Q5: You're working on the security team for an oil company whose website is frequently being defaced by an environmental group, which category of threat actors best describes them?
> - [ ] Script Kiddies
> - [ ] Environmental Hackers
> - [ ] Hacktivists
> - [ ] Hippy Hackers
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Hacktivists**
> </details>
