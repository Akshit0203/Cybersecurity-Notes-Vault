# Social Engineering — Notes & Check-In Quiz

## Key Terms

| Term                     | Meaning                                                              | Example                                                              |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Smishing**             | Phishing through **SMS/text messages**                               | "Your bank account is locked. Click this link."                      |
| **Vishing**              | Phishing through **voice/phone calls**                               | Attacker calls helpdesk and asks to reset MFA                        |
| **Spear Phishing** 🎯   | Highly **targeted and personalised phishing**                        | Email using details about someone's children                         |
| **Whaling** 🐋           | Spear phishing targeting a **high-value executive**                  | Fake email targeting a CFO/CEO                                       |
| **Pretexting** 🎭        | Creating a **fake story/identity** to obtain information or action   | "I'm from your bank; verify your account."                           |
| **Tailgating**           | Physically following an authorised person into a restricted area     | Balancing coffees and following someone through a door                |
| **Out-of-band verification** | Verifying a request using a **different communication channel** | Receiving email → calling boss on a known number to verify           |

### Out-of-Band Verification — Example

```
Email request
     ↓
Looks suspicious
     ↓
Call boss using known phone number
     ↓
Verify request
```

> **Out-of-band = Verify something using a different communication channel than the original request.**

**Email → Phone call** = Out-of-band verification ✅

### Whaling vs Spear Phishing

If a CFO receives a highly personalised phishing email, it could be described as **both** spear phishing (personalised) and whaling (targets an executive). However, the key differentiator in the course quiz is the **personalisation**, making **Spear Phishing** the best answer.

## Check-In Quiz

> [!question] Q1: A helpdesk worker receives a call from a cybercriminal asking them to reset the MFA on an account, this is an example of?
> - [ ] Smishing
> - [ ] Helphishing
> - [ ] Tishing
> - [ ] Vishing
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Vishing**
> </details>

> [!question] Q2: A CFO at a company receives an email pertaining to be from their child's school which contains specific details about their children causing them to fall for the phish, this is an example of?
> - [ ] Whaling
> - [ ] Spear Phishing
> - [ ] Pretexting
> - [ ] All of the above
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Spear Phishing**
> </details>

> [!question] Q3: An attacker waits by the door of a busy office entrance during the morning balancing a tray of coffees and easily follows in another employee even though they notice them, this is an example of?
> - [ ] Impersonation
> - [ ] Tailgating
> - [ ] Phishing
> - [ ] Sneaking
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Tailgating**
> </details>

> [!question] Q4: A customer support agent is on a call with what appears to be a customer, while they're waiting for the customer's website to load the customer asks the support agent in small talk what kind of computer they are using and where they are. Could this potentially be social engineering?
> - [ ] Yes
> - [ ] No
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Yes**
> </details>

> [!question] Q5: A receptionist at a company receives an email that pertains to be from her boss, the request in the email doesn't seem right so she calls him on the number from her contacts to verify, what is this an example of?
> - [ ] Phishing
> - [ ] Whaling
> - [ ] Fraud
> - [ ] Out-of-band verification
>
> <details><summary>Answer</summary>
> 
> **Out-of-band verification**
> </details>
