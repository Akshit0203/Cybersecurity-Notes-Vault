### Matt Brown

Matt has an awesome YouTube channel with copious videos showing hands on IoT and hardware hacking and tutorials. If you want to follow along he usually shows most or all of the steps and if you want to learn about hacking something, Matt probably has a video on it or something similar at least. He's also got a blog with great articles and lessons. Additionally Matt has an amazing Discord community called the IoT Hacker Hideout filled with people who are passionate about IoT hacking and spanning all experience levels. There are some incredibly talented hackers in there who love to help out and share their knowledge. They also run group hacking projects where the server picks an affordable IoT device and works together to test it. I'm a member and mod and hope to see you over there!

[https://www.youtube.com/@mattbrwn](https://www.youtube.com/@mattbrwn)

## Books

---

### Practical IoT Hacking

Excellent and comprehensive book that I reference frequently.

[https://nostarch.com/practical-iot-hacking](https://nostarch.com/practical-iot-hacking)

---

### Hardware Hackers Handbook

Great book, as the title suggests, more specifically focused on hardware hacking.

[https://nostarch.com/hardwarehacking](https://nostarch.com/hardwarehacking)

---

### Microcontroller Exploits

Just as the title says, if you're interested in the nitty gritty details about exploiting microcontrollers, then this book is for you.

[https://nostarch.com/microcontroller-exploits](https://nostarch.com/microcontroller-exploits)

---

### Mastering Embedded Linux Programming

This isn't specifically an IoT hacking course, however, in my opinion learning the ins and outs of embedded Linux is one of the most important things for IoT hacking. This book does an amazing job of explaining how embedded Linux works and how to build out your own embedded Linux firmware. Which I highly suggest you do!

[https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/mastering-embedded-linux-programming-9781789530384](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/mastering-embedded-linux-programming-9781789530384)

### Fuzzing Against the Machine

Along with having an awesome title, this book also does a great job at showing how to fuzz embedded devices by emulating them with QEMU and using AFL++.

[https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/fuzzing-against-the-machine-9781804614228](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/fuzzing-against-the-machine-9781804614228)
