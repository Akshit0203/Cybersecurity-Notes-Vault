### Introduction:
- It is a technique used to manipulate the employees in the org to gain unauthorized access to sensitive info and facilities.
- It exploits human psychology and deceive them to perform actions that will compromise the security.
- Here we are targeting the weakest link the security chain : Human element.
- As everyone using social media, its easy for attackers to target any employee in the world.
- Also they post some info about the, which is helpful for attackers during bruteforce.

### Steps to perform Phishing:
**Planning and Recon**:
- Research about target org and identify the potential targets, gather info about employees and understand org's protocol.
**Message Crafting**:
- Attackers create deceptive emails or messages and make it looks like it comes from their colleague or friend or from a trusted source.
**Delivery**:
- Attackers send phishing emails and also use some techniques to bypass email filters.
**Deception and Manipulation**:
- The phishing links will have malicious files and some fake logins, recipients will download and put creds.
**Exploitation**:
- After the victim download file,we have to gain access and identify vulnerability.
