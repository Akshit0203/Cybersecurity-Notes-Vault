# How the Brain Works: Neuroplasticity

Neuroplasticity - something you have done again and again so many times that it has now been embedded in your mind 
you don't need effort to do it now/again

can be anything from doing a certain kind of work to watching reels in the morning in bed

now you don't have to rewire them 
you just have to build new connections
